# Thread: Jones & the Midpoint

## 1. Evolution

**Oct 2022 — the inciting climb belongs to Julie, not Jones.** The very first notes on this thread have the *wrong* woman going up the hill: "Julie hitting a drone doesn't make sense, she needs to disappear quietly — she says she's going up for a word" (2022-10-27, 3c9251d2), refined the same day into the load-bearing decision "JONES NEEDS TO STOP HER DOING A STUPID BUT DRONE STOPS HER, SHE NEVER SEES JULIE COME BACK" (96e7e32b, `isDecision: true`) and "Too angry NOISE so she goes for a word" (f678b958). In this original conception Jones is the bystander who *fails* to stop Julie's reckless recon up to the mast, and Julie vanishes clean, off-page, forever. Companion notes the same day are pure craft ("Jones run — just not with sirens and that," cd228114) and theme ("All horrible shit happened after not when she voted," c46bde9e) rather than plot.

**Jan–Feb 2023 — the roles flip, and this is the real hinge of the thread.** "Julie is a watched dissident who is disappeared/abducted... Jones is a good replacement" (2023-01-03, 74ed4a12, `isDecision: true`) quietly reverses the October setup: Julie is no longer the one who climbs and vanishes under her own power, she is *taken*, and Jones is installed as her structural substitute — the new person the plot follows into danger. Everything from here on has *Jones* doing the hill-climbing/investigating that Julie did in the original notes. 2023-02-08's "Is it better if you don't know what happened to Jones? Yep." (9f4028ab, `isDecision: true`) then makes not-knowing itself the engine — withholding Jones's fate is what pulls John back into the story, which is a very different structural idea from October's "Julie never comes back" mystery, now redirected onto Jones.

**Spring 2023 — the codes/Reza/Palm machinery gets built around the death, and the self-edit that saves Jones's centrality.** 2023-04-03 (76ca0ffb) and 2023-04-21 (9bdc103e, `isDecision: true`) lock the mechanical sequence — John without codes, Palm's warning, Reza retrieving John, "Jones blow up," then getting the codes — that the later doctrine notes never revisit. In between, 2023-05-02's self-edit (2c279709, `isDecision: true`) is explicit and important: "Jones has to remain important because it's her that drives the start of the book." This is the author catching a drift toward treating Jones as a mere plot-device casualty and re-asserting her as the book's actual opening protagonist — validated later by the audit (see §3).

**Mid-2023 — a flash-forward opening is floated, then quietly abandoned.** "Start with Jones death?" (2023-07-13, 82d51ea3, `isDecision: true`) proposes opening the book with the death itself for pace. This is never picked up again, and it is directly superseded less than a year later by **2024-04-29's "LL JONES, THEN SHE DIES THEN ALL JOHN"** (e14a95b9, `isDecision: true`) — a committed *chronological* handover structure, Jones first, no flash-forward. The character-threads audit confirms the chronological version is what's on the page (Jones dominant 3–203, dies at 256, John takes over after), so the July 2023 flash-forward idea lost.

**Mid-to-late 2023 — texture accumulates but the author later judges it insufficient.** Costume (2023-04-15, 2f65e8b6), the metalhead vest (2023-05-03, b11d8a6d), the one-room house (2023-07-06, fa5d050b), full name Esmeralda Taliesin Jones (2023-08-09, 86729fc4), RZ status (2023-08-16) — all surface detail. Despite this, **2024-11-08 reverses the assumption that detail equals character**: "She's not interesting because you know nothing about her — Spartan wasn't the right word. Empty, maybe." (1ec5e71f, `isDecision: true`). This note explicitly supersedes the earlier belief that costume/name/house notes were doing enough work, and it precedes a run of interior, opinionated dialogue notes in 2025 (perpetual maybes, belief in something, dream-phonecalls, "biggest mistake I ever made was nothing") that read like the direct fix.

**Nov 2024 — Julie's fate is rewritten twice in twelve days, and never resettled.** 2024-11-11's "Jones finds Julie dead, surrounded by crow propaganda, just as others find her" (f8c8dee0, `isDecision: true`) gives Julie a confirmed, staged death. Twelve days later, 2024-11-23's detailed sequencing has Jones "spot warning messages... investigate... Julie didn't work out, I think we blew her mind" (45b93093, `isDecision: true`) — Julie's mind is *blown*, not her body killed; this is a different, incompatible fate written by the same author in the same month, and neither note retires the other. A year later, 2025-11-30's "Julie was their type, politically disgruntled, even if she was a bit too white" (a13c7e9f, `isDecision: true`) drifts back to the vaguer 2023-vintage framing — Julie as one of many "cleared" dissidents ("people don't just vanish anymore, not since the clearings") — without picking either the "found dead" or "mind blown" version. Three incompatible fates now sit in the notes unreconciled (see §3).

**Late 2024 — the doctrine starts to firm up around what the midpoint is *for*.** "They think Jones is a crow" (2024-11-07, 52d53a2a, `isDecision: true`), "no one knows [the Raven] died... only John and Vetko know the truth" (2024-11-10, a2039f9e, `isDecision: true`) and "Vetko decides not to kill Reza... She'll do" (2024-07-29, 93724e11, `isDecision: true`) all converge on the same idea already present in embryo at 2022-11-24's "When Jones dies — nothing personal" (8ba4354e, `isDecision: true`): Jones is killed because she is *available and sufficient*, not because she is significant to Vetko. "She'll do" is the sharpest, latest confirmation of the 2022 instinct — a rare case of a note two years later *validating* rather than revising the earliest one.

**2025 — craft notes on the death scene converge on restraint, right as a big, mythic alternative is also floated.** 2025-06-22's revision note is the clearest single craft decision in the whole thread: "Jones death is pretty good, need maybe a line to say she looked at Roger and then I thought there would be more, like whisper it not say it" (5ac2e9fd, `isDecision: true`) — this is the author naming the exact line and the exact register (whispered, not stated) months before the reader panel would split violently over that same line (see §3). Then **2025-12-07 goes the other way**: "it consumes your soul that's why it's so bad, it's going to eat everyone's brain," Jones's death rendered as an idyllic hallucination with "Jothas" intercut with her real walk to detonate (25b00c79, `isDecision: true`). This is a much bigger, more mythologized, more *explained* treatment of the death than "whisper it not say it" — a real tension inside the same year of notes.

**Jan 2026 — doctrine reins the drift back in.** The two extensive 2026-01-24 doctrine notes (3f5e8ef4, 9259906e, both `isDecision: true`) explicitly state "Jones's death is bureaucratic, not heroic," name "the Watch explosion" as "the load-bearing midpoint pivot," and lay down the rule that "the midpoint shift must never be softened, explained, or comforted afterward." Coming six weeks after the elaborate soul-consuming hallucination of 2025-12-07, this doctrine reads as the author catching the same kind of drift caught in 2023-09 on the Vetko thread: pulling back from myth/explanation toward the cold, administrative "nothing personal" register that was correct from the start. "John does not change the world. The world keeps changing around him" (9259906e) is the fullest, latest statement of what the handover is actually for.

## 2. The best decision

**The 2026-01-24 doctrine pair is the strongest, most complete version of this thread**, and it is where the author should build from. It is the only place that holds all of the following without contradiction: Jones as "the early emotional centre" whose death is a structural pivot, not a heroic sacrifice; the Watch explosion named explicitly as "the load-bearing midpoint pivot"; and the hard rule that the handover "must never be softened, explained, or comforted afterward" — which retroactively disciplines both the 2023-07 flash-forward wobble and the 2025-12-07 mythic-hallucination wobble.

But the doctrine is the *conclusion*, not the discovery — two earlier notes did the load-bearing work it's synthesizing, and are worth keeping visible as the plainer statements: **2022-11-24, "When Jones dies — nothing personal"** (8ba4354e) is the one-sentence logline the whole thread is really about, and it was right on the first try — everything from "She'll do" (2024-07-29) to "bureaucratic, not heroic" (2026-01-24) is that same idea getting sharper, never reversed. **2024-04-29, "ALL JONES, THEN SHE DIES THEN ALL JOHN"** (e14a95b9) is the structural decision that actually shipped — it beat 2023-07-13's flash-forward alternative and matches the manuscript's real shape per the character-threads audit.

The one place a later note is arguably worse than what came before: **2025-12-07's soul-consuming/"Jothas" hallucination** (25b00c79) is a more ambitious, more written scene than "whisper it not say it" (2025-06-22), but it pulls toward exactly the explained, mythologized treatment the January doctrine then rules out. Don't build the death scene from 25b00c79 without deliberately deciding to override the doctrine that supersedes it seven weeks later.

## 3. Against the audits

**(a) Notes that already answer a gap the audit flagged**

- **Stanley/Hard-Hat continuity.** Reader-report's honourable mentions ask the author to "flag the Stanley continuity reveal (187 → 273) so it doesn't read as coincidence." **2025-04-14's "She goes up the hill because she sees in the distance a hard hat or workmen"** (25ac7096) is exactly the connective tissue the audit wants — Jones's climb is triggered by *spotting* the Hard Hat figure, not coincidence. This just needs to be made legible on the page, not invented.
- **The Drone tape's existence and placement.** Character-threads flags Jones's 204–249 gap (offstage in the van between capture and detonation) as covered "only by the 'Set IT Free' interludes," and reader-report calls the Drone tape (411–418) the unanimous strongest dread in the book. **2024-09-18's "Don't see the second half of her being caught just the first"** (642e79b2, `isDecision: true`) is the note that explains *why* that gap exists and *why* it pays off later as footage — the audit's structural observation is something the author already decided on, not a hole to fill.
- **The "no quip after the peak" fix and the fight over 255–256's final line.** Reader-report's #2 top fix across all twelve reads is "no quip after the peak," and the single most contested beat in the whole manuscript is Jones's death line — "a proper gut-punch" to the comedy lifer, "the book flinching" to the literary reader. **2025-06-22's "need maybe a line to say she looked at Roger and then I thought there would be more, like whisper it not say it"** (5ac2e9fd) is the author's own prescription for exactly this problem, months before the panel split on it — the fix (make it quieter, not punchier) is already written down.
- **Jones's centrality pre-midpoint.** Character-threads measures it directly: "In snippets 1–160 Jones is on-page for roughly 120 snippets to John's ~95." **2023-05-02's self-edit, "Jones has to remain important because it's her that drives the start of the book"** (2c279709, `isDecision: true`), is the author deciding this in advance — the audit's measurement is confirmation of a note, not a discovery.
- **The Watch-explosion-as-pivot framing.** Character-threads' own dedicated section calls the handover abrupt, total, and agency-transferring. **2026-01-24's "the Watch explosion" as "the load-bearing midpoint pivot"** (9259906e) is the author naming, in almost the same language, what the audit independently reconstructed from the page.

**(b) Notes that contradict what the manuscript currently does**

- **The October 2022 Julie-climbs/Jones-fails-to-stop-her conception is reversed on the page.** 96e7e32b's "SHE NEVER SEES JULIE COME BACK" has *Jones* as the passive witness and *Julie* as the one who disappears clean. But character-threads shows the manuscript did the opposite: Jones is the one abducted on the hill (166–203, "captured 202–203"), while Julie is already offstage/catatonic in the van earlier (195, 198, 201). If this early note is ever cited as "how the hill scene works," it will contradict who is actually climbing and who is actually taken.
- **Julie's three incompatible fates.** 2024-11-11's "Jones finds Julie dead, surrounded by crow propaganda" (f8c8dee0) directly contradicts 2024-11-23's "Julie didn't work out, I think we blew her mind" (45b93093) — one kills Julie's body, the other breaks her mind and (by the phrasing) leaves her alive. Character-threads' own read of the page — Julie "catatonic in the van" at 195/198/201, fate after 235 "never resolved on-page" — lines up with the *mind-blown* version, not the *found-dead* version, which suggests f8c8dee0 was never actually carried onto the page and shouldn't be treated as canon alongside 45b93093.
- **The mythologized 2025-12-07 death sequence versus the 2026-01-24 doctrine.** "It consumes your soul... eat everyone's brain," rendered as an intercut hallucination with "Jothas" (25b00c79), is a heavily *explained*, mythic treatment. Six weeks later the doctrine states the handover "must never be softened, explained, or comforted afterward" and that Jones's death is "bureaucratic, not heroic" (3f5e8ef4). Reader-report's own description of the scene as it reads on the page — "the farce-into-bomb collision and the 'PART TWO' hard cut" — sounds like the terse, abrupt version won, not the hallucination. Treat 25b00c79 as superseded rather than as the operative death-scene design.
- **The garden "Pulpos" creature versus the finalized Harmony/mind-control mechanism.** 2023-02-08's "She thought she caught one in her garden and they were messing with her" (20790b27) implies a literal creature explanation for what's happening to Julie, which sits oddly against the psychoacoustic, mind-in-a-jar mechanism the it-brain notes settle into by 2023-04-29 ("IT WAS NEVER THERE," a6c84f6f) and 2025-12-07 (soul-consumption). If the manuscript's Harmony/IT mythology is the psychic-administrative one the doctrine describes, the literal garden-monster version is a different, uningested register.

**(c) Ideas that are stale / superseded / safe to bin**

- **2022-10-27's Julie-with-a-baseball-bat recon mission** (96e7e32b, f678b958, 3c9251d2) — fully superseded once 2023-01-03 makes Julie the one abducted and Jones her replacement (74ed4a12). The specific mechanics (baseball cover story, Jones failing to stop her) never recur after 2022 and contradict who does what on the page.
- **2023-07-13's "Start with Jones death?"** (82d51ea3) — a flash-forward opening idea that 2024-04-29's committed chronological structure (e14a95b9) replaced outright, and which the manuscript's actual shape (per character-threads) confirms was not used.
- **2024-11-11's "Jones finds Julie dead"** (f8c8dee0) — displaced twelve days later by 45b93093's incompatible "mind blown" version, and unsupported by the manuscript's own catatonic-Julie-in-the-van evidence. Keep 45b93093, retire this one, or explicitly reconcile them before drafting.
- **2023-07-26's "job interview with Vetko"** (d6d12a48) — an alternate-title-era ("Parliament of the wolf") doodle that never recurs in any later note; a dead branch.
- **2025-12-07's soul-consuming/"Jothas" hallucination** (25b00c79) — not bad material, but in direct tension with the doctrine that supersedes it (see §3b); shelve unless the author deliberately overrides the January 2026 "never explained" rule.
- **The early skeletal beat-lists** ("Jones go boom," 05333f24, 2022-10-28; "Jones blew up," f14bd472, 2023-08-01) — useful backbone but fully absorbed and made more granular by 2025-04-10's detailed sequencing (8feb2b17) and the 2026-01-24 doctrine; no new information left to mine from the early versions specifically.
