# Thread: Julie

## 1. Evolution

**27 Oct 2022 — Julie is the one who climbs the hill, and she comes home.** The thread starts as a scrappy, almost comic recon-mission plot. Julie "already knows what Harmony is doing" and "needs to disappear quietly" rather than attack a drone (3c9251d2); she goes up with a baseball bat because she's "too angry NOISE" (f678b958); Jones fails to stop her and the drone doesn't react — "JONES NEEDS TO STOP HER DOING A STUPID BUT DRONE STOPS HER, SHE NEVER SEES JULIE COME BACK" (96e7e32b, `isDecision: true`). Critically, in this original version **Julie survives the climb** — "Julie DOES however have a weapon after dark and Paul Bond is not happy about it" (355fddad, `isDecision: true`), Roger suspends her (0651d444, `isDecision: true`), and only afterward does she go dark — "Steering clear if drone saw her with a baseball bat she's fucked it" (50c3a181). The same day's worldbuilding ties her to the estate's texture ("There are still cocks spray painted everywhere," 2aba383e) and the to-do list "Fix Julie / Fix Palm / Shorten" (a0fafca5, `isDecision: true`) flags her arc as already broken. 28 Oct's full timeline (05333f24, `isDecision: true`) locks this into the book's backbone sequence.

**3 Jan 2023 — the frame flips from "reckless" to "targeted."** "Julie is a watched dissident who is disappeared/abducted... Jones is a good replacement" (74ed4a12, `isDecision: true`) quietly reverses October: Julie no longer climbs and returns under her own power, she is *taken*, and her removal is what installs Jones as her structural substitute. This is the real hinge of the whole thread — everything downstream treats Julie's disappearance as done to her, not chosen by her.

**Feb–Apr 2023 — a discarded byway, then the mystery-keeping doctrine.** 7 Feb's tumtums-interrogate-John scene is self-flagged and dead on arrival: "BAD WRONG NO DONT USE" (82027e22, `isDecision: true`) — the author catching their own bad idea in real time. The same day, Palm's forcing-function is settled: he tells the tumtums "John is looking into Julie (who'd reached them via Rosemary Doris)" (60567ca7, `isDecision: true`), and John is accused, "You're the only reason she was ever involved" (65646f1c). 8 Feb's "She thought she caught one in her garden and they were messing with her" (20790b27) plants a literal Pulpos-creature explanation for what's happening to Julie — a register that later mythology (the entity/sound mechanism, see below) will move away from. Then **15 Apr's decision is the load-bearing craft call for the entire thread**: "Find Julie don't say what happened til john sees the video leave some mystery" (84f228f1, `isDecision: true`). Everything from here on treats withheld information as the engine, not a hole to patch.

**1 May 2023 — the one clean, violent resolution the notes ever commit to.** "He has Roger kill Julie / Vetko thinks it awesome, so leaves Baker there and takes Roger" (15880667, `isDecision: true`) is the most concrete answer the author ever writes down: Julie is murdered, on Baker's order, by Roger. This is a real commitment — a named killer, a named order-giver, a consequence (Vetko's approval reshapes the Baker/Vetko/Roger triangle). Nothing this specific about her death recurs again in the notes.

**Nov 2023 – mid-2024 — mechanism note, then a reframed inciting act.** 13 Nov 2023's "She doesn't know she just hates the noise" (6164c7d1) is a durable mechanic: it lets Julie's POV/behaviour explain worldbuilding without spoiling the mystery the April decision protects. 18 Jun 2024's "She's picketing and decides to stay out after curfew as a real sign of protest" (5bd57392, `isDecision: true`) **quietly replaces the October 2022 inciting act.** The reason she's out past curfew is no longer a furtive armed recon mission with a bat and a cover story about baseball — it's an open act of civil disobedience. This is a tonal reversal (furtive/reckless → deliberate/political) that nothing in the notes flags explicitly as a supersession, but it cannot coexist with the bat-and-baseball mechanics without a rewrite.

**Nov 2024 — three incompatible fates in eighteen days.** 6 Nov's directive is explicit: "Don't send her into the in between / Go / Julie's first" (38af5c12, `isDecision: true`) — rejecting a liminal, unresolved fate and insisting Julie's storyline get priority. Five days later, **8db192e5 explores exactly the idea just rejected**: "What if Julie was up there right now, confused" (11 Nov) — a direct tension with 38af5c12, unflagged by the author. The same day, a different, more concrete fate is drafted: "Jones finds Julie dead, surrounded by crow propaganda, just as others find her" (f8c8dee0, `isDecision: true`) — Julie's body is found, which is why she's ostracised, and Jones's disbelief of the "crow" cover story is what sends her up the hill later. Twelve days after *that*, 23 Nov's detailed sequencing overwrites it again: "Julie didn't work out, I think we blew her mind" (45b93093, `isDecision: true`) — her mind is broken, not her body killed, by crow-disguised operatives near the masts; this is the version where Jones is then taken herself. **Three different fates — murdered by Roger (May 2023), found dead with crow propaganda (11 Nov 2024), mind-blown but alive (23 Nov 2024) — now sit unreconciled**, and the notes never retire any of them explicitly.

**Apr 2025 — the mythology finally lands on something specific, and it matches the page.** 12 Apr's entity/Stanley worldbuilding gives the clearest mechanism yet: the sound-based entity "wants to cross into human minds... but sometimes fries people, and Julie fought back, they removed her from the estate and now they need another victim" (a1e2d6e8, `isDecision: true`) — this explicitly sets Jones up as that next victim, tying directly back to Jan 2023's "Jones is a good replacement." Two days later, 14 Apr's note is the most granular and operationally specific of the whole thread: "the entity is put in her head and set free but doesn't work properly; she's held unconscious in a van while they test locality... jones sees her when she is captured" (c5c2b66a, `isDecision: true`). This is the version — captured, unconscious, held in a van — and it is the version that reads as closest to whatever made it onto the page (see §3). The same week, 25ac7096 gives Jones a concrete visual trigger for going up the hill: spotting "a hard hat or workmen" in the distance.

**Nov 2025 – Jan 2026 — the fate question is dropped in favour of political framing.** 30 Nov 2025's decision that direct backstory exposition is fine gives Jones a line contextualising Julie without resolving her fate mechanically: "people don't just vanish anymore, not since the clearings," and "Julie was their type, politically disgruntled, even if she was a bit too white" (a13c7e9f, `isDecision: true`). This drifts back toward the vaguer Jan 2023 "watched dissident" framing rather than picking between the May 2023 / Nov 2024 / Apr 2025 mechanical fates. 13 Jan 2026's final note, "She'd been protesting against masts for generations" (daf8101a), completes this drift: the latest word on Julie is about her *identity* (lifelong protester) rather than her *fate*. The fate question the author kept re-answering all thread — murdered? found dead? mind-blown? captured-in-a-van? — is, as of the newest note, left open again.

## 2. The best decision

**The strongest, most complete version of this thread is the April 2025 pair — a1e2d6e8 (12 Apr) and c5c2b66a (14 Apr) — not the newest notes.** Together they are the only version of Julie's fate that (a) gives a mechanism (the sound-entity, fried minds, "Julie fought back"), (b) gives a concrete physical state (captured, unconscious, held in a van while "they test locality"), (c) gives a witness and a payoff beat ("Jones sees her when she is captured"), and (d) explains *why the plot needs Jones next* — she is literally the next candidate after Julie "didn't work out." No other note in the thread does all four at once. It is also, notably, the version that most closely matches how the manuscript audit describes Julie actually appearing on the page: captured and unconscious in a van (see §3).

The 15880667 "Roger kills Julie" decision (1 May 2023) was a strong, clean beat in isolation — a named killer with a motive — but it was superseded rather than built on: nothing after 2023 keeps Julie dead by Roger's hand specifically, and it cannot be reconciled with the Apr 2025 "held in a van" version without deciding Roger was the one who *captured* rather than *killed* her.

The two Nov 2024 fates (f8c8dee0's "found dead," 45b93093's "mind blown") were drafting churn on the way to the Apr 2025 version, not improvements on it — keep 45b93093's "mind blown, not killed" logic (it's compatible with the Apr 2025 van scene) and retire f8c8dee0's "found dead."

The most recent notes (a13c7e9f, daf8101a) are not an improvement on the fate question — they're a retreat from it into characterisation. They're valuable for texture (see §4) but should not be read as superseding the Apr 2025 decision; if anything, they leave a gap the Apr 2025 notes already filled and should be reunited with it, not treated as the latest and therefore final word.

## 3. Against the audits

**(a) Notes that already answer a gap the audit flagged**

- **W2 — "Julie Cooper's fate after ~256... never resolved on-page... Decide and leave one signpost."** The notes already contain the decision the audit is asking for. 12–14 Apr 2025 (a1e2d6e8, c5c2b66a) gives exactly the missing mechanism: she "fought back" against the entity, was "removed from the estate," is "held unconscious in a van while they test locality," and "Jones sees her when she is captured." This lines up with the audit's own description of the page — "captured and unconscious in the van (195)... a fugitive per the Watch (63, 364)... never resolved on-page." The manuscript apparently already dramatizes the *capture*; what's missing is the *signpost* the author's own 15 Apr 2023 decision calls for — "don't say what happened til John sees the video" (84f228f1). The video is the payoff the notes promise and the manuscript has not yet delivered.
- **W9 — the unnamed possessed-woman POV ("Set IT Free," 213–234) — "confirm she isn't read as Julie (already in the van at 195)."** 12 Apr 2025's a1e2d6e8 directly answers this: Julie "fought back" and was removed, so "now they need another victim" — meaning the entity moves on to someone *else*. The unnamed "Set IT Free" POV is very likely that next victim, not Julie, exactly as the audit suspected. This is confirmation the audit's guess was right, sourced straight from the author's own note.
- **457's "Roger's shame-Julie" echo.** The audit logs this only as an unresolved echo. 1 May 2023's "He has Roger kill Julie" (15880667) is the one note that gives Roger a direct, personal reason to carry guilt about Julie specifically — though see (b) below, it needs reconciling with the later "captured, not killed" mechanism rather than taken as-is.

**(b) Notes that contradict what the manuscript currently does**

- **15880667's "Roger kills Julie" contradicts the van-capture version the manuscript apparently uses.** If snippet 195 has Julie "captured and unconscious in the van," she is not dead by Roger's hand in the way 1 May 2023 specifies. Either Roger's killing was cut in favour of the entity/capture mechanism, or Roger did the *capturing* rather than the killing and the note's language needs updating — but as written, the note and the page disagree about whether Julie is alive.
- **The October 2022 "Julie survives, comes home armed, gets suspended" arc is reversed on the page.** 355fddad and 0651d444 have Julie returning from the hill climb, getting suspended by Roger, and only going quiet afterward. But the audit's read of the manuscript has her disappearing for good with no on-page resolution after ~256 (W2) — the "comes home, causes fallout, then goes dark" beat isn't what's on the page. This early note should not be cited as how her disappearance actually plays out.
- **76ca0ffb's "Cesira Brogue's 112-hour premiership (a Liz Truss gag)" adds a third, worse number to E1.** The audit already flags a hard contradiction between 22 days (snippets 73, 240) and 99 days (snippet 85) for Cesira's time in power. This Julie-thread note — dated 3 Apr 2023 — independently proposes 112 hours (≈4.7 days), a third figure that agrees with neither manuscript number. If this note ever gets drafted into prose, it becomes a third contradiction on top of E1 rather than a fix for it.
- **20790b27's literal garden-creature explanation sits oddly against the later entity/sound mythology.** "She thought she caught one in her garden and they were messing with her" (8 Feb 2023) implies a physical Pulpos-type creature. The mechanism the thread eventually settles on (Apr 2025's sound-based entity that crosses into minds) is psychic/administrative, not a physical creature in a garden. If both are meant to be true, a line is needed connecting them; if not, the garden version is a different, uningested register and should be dropped.

**(c) Ideas that are stale / superseded / safe to bin**

- **The 2022-10-27 baseball-bat recon mission and its aftermath** (96e7e32b, 355fddad, 0651d444, 50c3a181, f678b958) — fully superseded once 3 Jan 2023 makes Julie the one abducted rather than the one who acts and returns (74ed4a12). Keep the *texture* (estate decay, "cocks spray painted everywhere") but not the plot mechanics.
- **82027e22's tumtums-interrogate-John scene** — self-flagged "BAD WRONG NO DONT USE" the day it was written. Bin outright; the author already did this for you.
- **11 Nov 2024's "Jones finds Julie dead, surrounded by crow propaganda"** (f8c8dee0) — displaced twelve days later by 45b93093's incompatible "mind blown, not killed" version, and superseded again five months later by the more specific Apr 2025 van-capture mechanism. Retire this one specifically; it's the weakest of the three Nov 2024–Apr 2025 fates.
- **11 Nov 2024's "up there right now, confused" musing** (8db192e5) — explores the exact "in between" fate that 38af5c12 rejected five days earlier. Given the Apr 2025 notes settle on a concrete, physical (not liminal) fate, this musing is a dead branch.
- **12 Apr 2023's "blows up / never found" fork** (45a74df9) — both options were superseded by the more specific Apr 2025 "captured, held in a van" resolution; neither "blows up" nor a clean "never found" survives once a concrete mechanism exists.
- **22 May 2023's "An arsehole disappearance"** (c62e6d41) — a bare fragment with no follow-through in any later note; nothing to mine.

## 4. Worth holding

- **15 Apr 2023 — "Find Julie don't say what happened til john sees the video leave some mystery."** (84f228f1) The single craft decision the whole thread depends on. It's also the note that names the exact fix for the audit's W2 gap: the video is the signpost that's owed.
- **13 Nov 2023 — "She doesn't know she just hates the noise."** (6164c7d1) A durable, still-useful mechanic for letting Julie's behaviour carry worldbuilding without spoiling the mystery — compatible with every later fate the thread considered.
- **12 Apr 2025 — "Julie fought back, they removed her from the estate and now they need another victim."** (a1e2d6e8) The mechanism note. Explains her removal, ties directly to Jones being "a good replacement" (74ed4a12, Jan 2023), and answers the audit's W9 ambiguity about the unnamed "Set IT Free" POV.
- **14 Apr 2025 — "jones sees her when she is captured."** (c5c2b66a) The most concrete, page-matching version of Julie's fate in the whole thread — held unconscious in a van, discovered by Jones. Build any resolution scene from this note, not from the May 2023 or Nov 2024 alternatives.
- **6 Nov 2024 — "Don't send her into the in between / Julie's first."** (38af5c12) A guardrail worth keeping visible so the thread doesn't drift back toward the liminal "up there, confused" idea it already rejected once.
- **30 Nov 2025 — "Julie was their type, politically disgruntled, even if she was a bit too white."** (a13c7e9f) The best available characterisation line — gives Julie political weight and a place in the "clearings" mythology. Should be paired with the Apr 2025 fate mechanism, not treated as a replacement for it.
- **13 Jan 2026 — "She'd been protesting against masts for generations."** (daf8101a) The latest and most durable one-line identity for Julie; consistent with the Jun 2024 curfew-protest reframing (5bd57392) and worth keeping as the throughline for who she is, independent of how her fate finally resolves.
