# Thread: The Raven = Erica / Beck Avon

## 1. Evolution

**The core duality (Nov 2022) never changed, but its mechanism did.** The thread opens with the founding conceit, stated twice on **2022-11-11**: John is a man hollowed out by tragic loss ("John blard was what happened when love ends tragically," d8a03d3c) and the decision that there are "**One where she died and one where he lies about it**" (826ba63c). That's the whole tenet in embryo — two truths, one dead woman. But note the verb: *he lies*. Early-thread John is imagined as an active deceiver. By **2024-11-10** (a2039f9e) the mechanism has quietly shifted from deception to structural ignorance: "**no one knows she died, she disappeared and started a terror group, only John and Vetko know the truth**" — the public doesn't know because the state itself never found out, not because John is running a con. This matches what the manuscript actually does (s337, s546 — Vetko's rage only works if the Realists never learned she died in one of their own hospitals) and quietly retires the "he lies about it" framing as too active/noir for what the book became.

**Grief-voice material (Nov 2022 – early 2025) is where most of the real work happened**, and it barely changed in kind, only accumulated: Reza's consolation and "**she didn't really like me much by the end, we were sort of together because we had to be**" (2022-11-18, f77d2384) shows up on the page nearly verbatim at s528. The photo-ritual, the "good days," the "funny thing, perspective" rug, the vanilla lotion, "imaginary dream dead wife," "she can't have happened" — from **2023-02-01 through 2023-08-10** — is a sustained, consistent voice that the audit confirms landed almost intact in s525-528, s179-183. This is the thread's most stable, least-revised material.

**Vetko's motive for caring about the Raven matured through three distinct passes.** First (**2023-01-20**, a9fb3ab9): simple and romantic — "**He wants a time back when Beck loved him**." Second (**2023-02-14 / 2023-02-27**): reframed as political/existential — he's "so scared of being left behind that he changes the world so he isn't" (23de4e16), and contempt-as-respect (915e14ae). Third and most sophisticated (**2024-09-22**, 5cc13844): a full meta-synthesis — Vetko knows he's in a story, his "entire personality is not wanting to become irrelevant — dead people don't matter" — so he sets out to change not just her death but *his own*. This last version doesn't contradict the earlier two so much as absorb them: wanting Beck back *is* the fear of irrelevance. The **2024-11-18** note (e7f2cc6e) then retroactively answers *why* he ever had her to lose: "**the whole first story is their break up and her falling for Vetko Block**" — a plot beat the earlier motive-notes assumed but never stated.

**The cause of Erica's death was rewritten at least three times, and the notes never fully reconciled with each other.** (1) **2023-04-22** (dceacf90): "**Not pandemic choked hospitals, tumult fucked she dies not long after the tumult ends because of what baker did to her**" — Baker/Tumult as proximate cause. (2) **2023-10-03** (02b0b092) then states the Pulpos "infested half the population... after she died" — Pulpos placed *after* her death. (3) **2025-04-14** (1d09f551) reverses that: a self-critique fixing the timeline by having her die *because* "**She couldn't go hospital because of the pulpos**" — Pulpos now has to be happening *at/before* her death, not after. That's a direct contradiction between 02b0b092 (2023) and 1d09f551 (2025) that the author never went back to close — and it's the exact same pinch the prequel-bible independently flags as broken ("Pulpos accelerated the zoning... doesn't fit in the time line," bible §2a / note s631). Then a fourth pass, the latest and richest (**2026-01-16**, 4c806c54): "**IT knows her because IT consumed her — first victim, kind of, a worm of sound**" — recasting her death again, this time as IT/ThinkTank predation via frequency, which could actually *reconcile* the UV5-virus cover story with the "eldritch, no virus" idea the bible flags as unresolved (open question #20) — IT's "worm of sound" could be what UV5 actually was. This is the strongest, latest layer, but the author has not gone back to retire the Baker/Pulpos versions underneath it.

**A discarded plot mechanism:** **2023-04-04** (49c25414) — "Beck like MTG for vetko? She gets him out but accidentally creates a shit storm — she causes the tumult" — has Beck springing Vetko from custody and accidentally triggering the Tumult. This never recurs in any later note (2024, 2025, 2026 notes about the Tumult's origin are silent on it), and it flatly contradicts the Tumult's established on-page trigger (Cesira's 22 days + the NIMBY rebellion riding Vetko, s73-75) — nothing about a jailbreak. Read as an abandoned idea, quietly dropped rather than revised.

**The mantle-transfer mechanic — arguably the hardest problem in this thread — was solved late.** Early notes (2022-12-01, d8639267: "Voice at first is him looking / Voice after is her") gesture at a device without a story reason for it. It isn't until **2025-09-21** (5f888a90) that the mechanism gets a causal explanation: an intended "sacrificial political assassination" (to strip and absorb main-character-hood) goes wrong — she dies alongside John instead — and that failure/anticlimax is precisely why the transfer left no trace on the page. This directly answers the prequel-bible's flagged problem that "the mantle transfer... left zero trace in book 2's death sequence" (bible §1.12, §4 item 12).

**Series structure crystallized very late and superseded everything vaguer that came before it.** **2023-05-15** (42a26894) floated "the last book... told as John watches Erica die, the tumult happens around them" — which can't be literally true against the fixed timeline (Tumult ~10y before book 2, her death ~8y before) unless read as a non-literal/replayed framing. **2023-06-15** (acbe00a6) added the time-traveler idea. Both are superseded by the clean, concrete map laid down on **2026-01-18** (c2063651): book 2 (prequel) = Vetko trying to save her; book 3 = flips to Beck as protagonist, John dies; book 4 = finale, Beck or both die, no mains left. Six days later, **2026-01-24** (9259906e) delivers what reads as the thread's governing doctrine, stated flatly: "**John does not change the world. The world keeps changing around him**" and the explicit rule that "his real past (Doug Goode, Raven) belongs only in the prequel" — which is, almost word for word, the premise the audit was written to test.

## 2. The best decision

**The 2026-01-24 doctrine note (9259906e)** is the strongest and most authoritative version of this thread, specifically the clause: *"his real past (Doug Goode, Raven) belongs only in the prequel."* It is the latest full-scope statement, it resolves the tension every earlier note was circling (how much of the Raven truth John/the book carries vs. defers), and it matches — almost exactly — the operating assumption the manuscript audit itself uses to test the tenet ("This book never states it"). Paired with it, **2025-09-21's mantle-transfer fix (5f888a90)** is the best solution to the thread's hardest mechanical problem (how the transfer happens invisibly), and **2026-01-18's series map (c2063651)** is the best structural container for the whole arc. Together these three late-2025/2026 notes are better than anything that came before them — not because the earlier grief material was wrong (it wasn't; most of it is already on the page and shouldn't be touched), but because only the late notes actually solve *why* the concealment works and *where* the truth eventually surfaces.

## 3. Against the audits

- **2025-09-21 (5f888a90)** — "Have the main character die for him and he absorbs the main character hood" — directly answers prequel-bible §4 item 12 / §1.12: "the mantle transfer... left zero trace in book 2's death sequence" and "must stage a transfer that book 2 can retroactively contain without a single altered sentence." The botched-assassination framing explains the zero-trace requirement causally instead of just asserting it.
- **2024-11-18 (e7f2cc6e)** — "the whole first story is their break up and her falling for vetko block" — directly answers prequel-bible §5 Open Question #13: "What Vetko wanted from/with Beck — love, ownership, the mantle itself... or the only person who ever treated him as real." The note supplies the missing romantic history the audit says is still undecided.
- **2023-08-07 (8fa523c1)** — "Flicker of raven face at the denunciation" — this is the author's own source for what the bible lists as a still-unexecuted planned seed (note s632, bible §1.12 and §4 item 19). The author already wrote the idea down; it just hasn't been drafted into the manuscript. **2025-11-10 (cfdd0c38)** extends it into the actual ending scene ("a flash of raven hair" on the flickering screen as John frees IT) — so the seed now has a home.
- **2026-01-16 (4c806c54)** — "IT knows her because IT consumed her — first victim, kind of, a worm of sound" — speaks directly to prequel-bible §5 Open Question #20 (UV5 virus vs. "no virus, only tentacles and eldritch weirdness," an unresolved tension the author flagged in their own note s637). This is a candidate resolution the audit doesn't yet know exists.
- **2023-06-15 (acbe00a6)** — the time-traveler note is the direct source of prequel-bible note s626, which the bible treats as a load-bearing, still-active constraint ("apparent in the first one but not the second"). The thread confirms this was a deliberate, repeated decision (also present in embryonic form at 2022-12-01, d8639267, and restated 2024-06-21, ce5e464d), not a one-off spark — worth treating as firm canon rather than a stray note.

**(b) Notes that contradict what the manuscript currently does**

- **2022-11-11 (826ba63c)** — "One where she died and one where he lies about it." The manuscript's actual mechanism (per audit §3/§4) is that the *state* never learned she died — not that John is lying to anyone on the page. This original framing is superseded by the 2024-11-10 "no one knows" version and should not be read as current.
- **2023-10-03 (02b0b092)** vs. **2025-04-14 (1d09f551)** — the Pulpos are placed *after* her death in the first note and *before/during* it in the second. Only one can be true, and the audit's own open question #19 (prequel-bible §5, "the Pulpos date... doesn't fit in the timeline") shows this was never actually settled — these two notes are the unreconciled evidence of that.
- **2023-04-04 (49c25414)** — Beck springing Vetko and accidentally causing the Tumult contradicts the manuscript's established trigger (Cesira's 22 days + NIMBY rebellion, s73-75, bible §1.2-1.3). Never repeated in any later note; treat as dead.
- **2023-01-20 (77ab812b)** — "kill code" implanted in John and Erica, known only to Elleray Palm. Neither audit file surfaces any on-page trace of a shared kill-code device (Palm's on-page role per the bible is the "recognition liturgy," s96-97 — a different mechanism). This may belong to the separate it-brain thread's own audit, but as far as raven-erica.md and prequel-bible.md are concerned, it's unconfirmed and possibly abandoned.
