# Thread: Music & Reza

## 1. Evolution

**2022-10-27 → 2023-06 (comic voice first, theme second).** The thread opens as pure character-voice collection: "Absolutely zorbing his tits off" (2022-10-27), "Absolutely biffo man" (2022-11-26), "Shop was too heavy" (2023-01-09), "I've got a rahted idea" (2023-03-09), "Man fuck words that don't sound like what they mean" (2023-03-30), "We not deal with dem ting" (2023-04-15). Reza is being built as a joke-delivery engine and a slang generator (2023-06-22: "He starts talking roadman and John can't understand him"). Music in this phase is mostly texture — "journey songs" for a montage (2022-11-19), "a hellacious techno dance groove" (2023-01-13) — not yet load-bearing.

**2022-11-02 → 2023-07 (the fatalism/grief layer arrives underneath the jokes).** In parallel, a second, heavier register opens almost immediately: "I'm more of an everybody's got to die type person" (2022-11-02), Reza consoling John about Erica (2022-11-18: "She didn't really like me much by the end"), and by 2023-05-30 and 2023-07-11 this becomes the thread's real spine — "Because the only thing I have left now is the fact that I'm alive and I'm scared Reza" and "What if I explore that grief and it turns out she wasn't very important at all." **2023-12-26 is the hinge**: "You take the piss out of things that make you feel" — the author turns the comic voice built in 2022–23 into something *examined*, retroactively reframing all the earlier banter as a defense mechanism rather than just color.

**2023-09-04 → 2026-01-10: a reversal on John's illegal music.** On 2023-09-04, the note says "He only listens to ska, because that's the only good music still allowed" — John positioned as compliant, just a ska fan. This is quietly **reversed** by 2026-01-10 (9432dde8): "He denies his nostalgia, in a way it's as bad, he pretends it never happened" — now John's illegal stash is *not* comforting old music but "strange, dated new material," and the point is psychological (denial), not taste. The later note is sharper and is the one that matches the manuscript's actual post-2001 illegal-mixtape canon; the 2023-09-04 "ska is all that's allowed" idea should be treated as superseded.

**2024-06-03 → 2024-12-24: naming the problem without solving it.** "Elevator" (2024-06-03) flags a *placement* decision — "Yime for j9hn and reza ro be beat friends" — but not a mechanism. By 2024-12-24 the author admits the mechanism is still missing: "Got to be a thing that binds them." This is a full year and a half after the relationship started generating pages of banter, and the note is effectively conceding that none of it has coalesced into an actual bond-object yet.

**2025-06-22: the pruning turn.** After three years of accumulating one-off jokes, the author reverses course on quantity: "need maybe... cut excessive Family-Guy-style pub jokes... Reza-and-John/John-not-going-home material [is] working." This explicitly deprioritizes the joke-fragment pile from 2022–2023 in favor of the load-bearing relationship material.

**2026-03-28: the resolution.** "Music has to be the thing" (6147314a) finally answers the 2024-12-24 question directly and explicitly: music, not banter, not slang, not shared danger, is declared the connective tissue of John and Reza's friendship. Everything after this date — the coded pre-Doris exchange (2026-05-30), Reza's fake "Mini Moog" synth persona (2026-06-01) — reads as execution of that directive: giving Reza his own invented relationship to music (a fifteen-year-old's fib that stuck) to make the bond reciprocal rather than John-sided.

**Net arc:** comic-voice accumulation (2022–23) → an emotional register grows underneath it, unlabeled (2022–23) → the joke-voice gets self-aware and thematized (2023-12) → a false lead on John's music taste gets corrected (2023-09 → 2026-01) → the author names the structural gap (2024-12) → prunes the noise (2025-06) → finally declares the fix (2026-03) and starts building toward it (2026-05/06).

## 2. The best decision

**6147314a, 2026-03-28 — "Music has to be the thing."** This is the strongest and correct version of the thread, and it is *late*, not early: everything before it (three-plus years of Reza banter, slang, and one-off jokes) generated texture and voice but never resolved what the relationship was actually built on. The 2024-12-24 note ("Got to be a thing that binds them") shows the author already sensed the gap for over a year before naming the answer. This is also, independently, the exact diagnosis the manuscript audit reaches — see below. The follow-through notes (coded music exchange before seeing Doris, 2026-05-30; Reza's fake teenage synth persona, 2026-06-01) are the right next steps but are not yet fully realized scene material — the decision is sound, the execution is in progress.
