# Thread: IT / Brain-in-Tank

## 1. Evolution

**2022-10-27 — the crows are cleared out of the way first.** Before It exists at all, the author rules out a competing monster: "Not crows, the crows are all in prison and being brainwashed." This matters because it frees the ThinkTank to be the sole hidden antagonist — the crows are downgraded to a decoy faction from day one.

**2022-10-28 — the founding decision, made in one day.** Three notes land within hours of each other and set the entire thread's spine before anything else exists: the ThinkTank is "literally a brain in a big tank" and "its escaping" (28bbab6c); the Watch's death is "set up for them to die to make harmony a bigger deal, think of it as an advertising campaign" (b5562e2d); and Palm hands John the codes as Jones dies (d0d929e1). Note the earliest framing of the brain is **an active escapee** — an agent breaking out on its own — which is a materially different mechanism from where the thread ends up (see 2025-11-10 below, where a human, John, releases it). This is the thread's first supersession, and it takes three years to resolve.

**Nov–Dec 2022 — texture before mechanism.** "Social lobotomy" (2022-11-02), the "crocodile" inside man (2022-11-24), and Baker's "woman weren't sexually activated at first, we did that to you" (2022-12-18) are all possession/control-adjacent color with no unifying theory yet. 2022-12-25's "It got too future so they got rid of it all, ai and all that" is the first-draft origin story for why an AI-run world would still need a brain in a jar — a placeholder that gets rewritten twice more (see 2023-04-09 and 2023-05-05).

**Jan 2023 — cipher, kill-codes, and a false start.** 2023-01-17's "the second ingredient of every curry spells a word" is the literal seed of the manuscript's govchat curry-spam cipher (matches the audit's decoded strings almost exactly in spirit). 2023-01-20 commits to John and Erica both having an implanted "kill code," known only to Palm — load-bearing and never contradicted later. But 2023-01-29's cover story that the Pulpos incident is "a mass hallucination" is quietly **overwritten** three months later by 2023-04-19's "Actually baker fucking with eldritch shit" being the real cause, officially blamed on "a foreign attack" instead — two different cover stories for the same event, and the second, not the first, is the one carried forward into 2023-07-05's firebombing/acid backstory.

**Jan–Feb 2023 — three competing origin stories for the collapse.** 2023-01-26 gives "chucked up a massive blimp full of surveillance drones and closed the borders" (AI took everyone's jobs). 2023-02-10 gives engineered "Trugs" (slugs) that "invaded half the publics brains and reduced the population by 47%." Neither Trug nor blimp-and-borders survives past this point — both are quietly dropped in favor of the war narrative below. 2023-02-07 is explicitly self-rejected by the author mid-note: "**BAD WRONG NO DONT USE**" — a kidnap/interrogation draft that never resurfaces.

**March 2023 — the monster is chosen and the mechanism starts to cohere.** 2023-03-06 decides definitively: "No virus, just tentacles and eldritch weirdness" — this is the note that locks Pulpos-not-plague. The same day, three notes build the sound mechanism in parallel: two songs combined make a controlling "symphony" (347cab6d), Baker's constant humming to "tune his brain" (f9b63319), and weaponizing a dog's devotion "at will, for everyone" (04a65dcd) — these three fuse into what becomes Harmony. 2023-03-18 gives the Pulpos their reveal scene ("they made it very clear they were eldritch overlords and nobody listened").

**April 2023 — the ThinkTank absorbs the eldritch material.** 2023-04-09's "Ai was all shut down except the think tank — it's the think tank they're taking out" **replaces** the Jan blimp/Trug origin stories with a cleaner one: only the ThinkTank survived the AI purge. 2023-04-13 gives it hardware — "Alien brain thing / Controls the drones / Harmony masts are amplifiers drones the speakers." 2023-04-22's "pulpifier" checkpoint (frequencies burn out the Pulpos) starts braiding Pulpos-defense technology into the same frequency science that will become Harmony.

**May 2023 — the reveal that unifies two separate monsters.** This is the thread's real turning point. 2023-05-05 states, flatly: "The thinktank is an eldritch brain in a jar" and explicitly rules out a separate "eldritch horrors ministry" — **superseding** March's "eldritch overlords" cosmology by folding it directly into the ThinkTank rather than keeping it as a second entity. The next day, 2023-05-06 names the pun that makes the fusion legible: "Bow to IT It's both an eldritch horror and then it department." 2023-05-13 supplies the missing science: "if you splice their [Pulpos'] brain frequency with ours you get the most pliable, most aggressive soldiers possible" — this is the mechanism that makes Harmony, the Pulpos, and the ThinkTank one system rather than three coincidental ideas.

**May–July 2023 — consolidation and Baker's slow disappearance into It.** 2023-05-20's "It could be how the pulpos were defeated, and now they want to change frequency. It's a mongoose" deepens the frequency lore. 2023-07-20's "You left me alone with it, it was all I had left" is a near-verbatim match for the manuscript's own Baker line (the audit's snippet 546) — this note is very likely its direct source. 2023-07-25 places the ThinkTank physically: "the thinktank bottom floor is psychoacoustics where all the weird shit happen," replacing any notion of a separate Peace Ministry department.

**Aug 2023 — a contradiction enters the notes and is never resolved.** 2023-08-07 states the ThinkTank is "the only thing separate from the Peace Ministry" — this directly contradicts 2023-07-25 (ThinkTank occupies the Peace Ministry's bottom floor) and is the same tension the tenet audit independently flags between its snippet 468 and scratch 632. The notes reproduce the manuscript's unresolved contradiction rather than solving it.

**Sept 2023 — the "big brain in a jar" line is spoken, not just noted.** 2023-09-03's "Big brain in a jar" (4ea0c9c9) is framed as dialogue ("Reza reveals to someone"), and the same day, "The IT department, no one really knows what they do down there but they run the whole show" (bf6e94d0) gives the department its public-facing mystique — both read as first drafts of manuscript-ready lines, not just worldbuilding memos.

**Sept 2023–Jan 2024 — the depopulation myth gets a fourth version.** 2023-09-26's "They caused depopulation — zombies kindve" attributes mass death to the Pulpos directly, on top of the already-dropped Trugs (Feb) and now-alongside 2023-05-05's earlier (undated in this thread but referenced) AI-war framing. By late 2023 the notes are carrying three unreconciled mass-death causes (Trugs, AI war, Pulpos-zombies) with no note explicitly retiring the other two — worth flagging as a live inconsistency, not a solved one.

**Dec 2023 — the Harmony/Pulpos trade-off gets its final shape.** 2023-12-12: "Harmony being switched off releases them [the Pulpos]." 2023-12-26 refines the ethics: "pulpos stopped the immigrants, the ends justify the means." This pairing — Harmony as the lesser evil that keeps a worse evil contained — becomes the thread's central irony and is never contradicted afterward.

**2024 — the mechanism becomes textual, and Baker's leash gets explicit.** 2024-01-14's "it infects radio waves nearby which is why it's in the chat" is the first note to explain **why** the govchat spam is corrupted at all — a direct, previously-missing answer to the mechanism behind the manuscript's cipher texts. The same day, "Baker becomes his vessel" (990e630e) restates 2022's founding idea as settled fact. 2024-10-06 complicates the power structure: "Baker isn't bothered, felicity basically makes the decisions" — Baker is not simply It's puppet, he's also disengaged from human politics entirely, which the audit's Baker material doesn't yet show.

**Nov 2024 — the reveal is restated at maximum clarity, and Julie is wired directly into the mechanism.** 2024-11-20's "it's the think tank trying to communicate it infects everything around it, baker has learnt to amplify the voice... to make people do what he wants" is the single clearest statement yet of how the ThinkTank, the govchat corruption, and Baker's control of Harmony all connect. 2024-11-25 doubles down: "At the end of the rabbit hole there is a machine, but there's also a big fucking brain in a jar," and clarifies the chain of command: Baker "gets orders from the think tank through in his office."

**2025 — Julie, Erica, and Baker's motive all get folded into It directly.** 2025-04-12: the entity "wants to cross into human minds via sound but sometimes fries people," and Julie "fought back" and was removed — this is the first note to make Julie a direct casualty of the ThinkTank's mechanism rather than just a disappeared dissident. 2025-02-13 gives Baker's actual downfall a cause: "Vetko brainwashes Baker with the IT/Harmony entity, Baker sets it free... and it eats him" — a plot beat, not just theme.

**Oct–Nov 2025 — the author self-audits and locks the ending.** 2025-10-26 (0ecc8258) is the author doing, in miniature, exactly what the tenet audit later does: "Thinktank isn't in it at all — big brain in a jar remember, only AI allowed, controls everything — the big brain in a jar needs to be released" — plus a directive to unify terminology across oohashi/harmony-node/pulpos/octopi/Ministry-of-Peace/ThinkTank. This is immediately followed by the ending mechanics: 2025-11-10's "John set IT free" (cfdd0c38) **reverses** 2022-10-28's original premise that It escapes on its own — now a human, unknowingly, does the releasing. The same week, "since we moved it the results are off the charts but less shit" (adef6e4c) confirms the brain was physically relocated *into* the ThinkTank rather than being a separate thing the ThinkTank merely houses — this resolves the Aug 2023 "separate from Peace Ministry" contradiction in the notes' own favor, by making the brain something that was moved, not something eternally fixed in one location.

**Nov–Dec 2025 — Baker's motive and Jones's death both get their final, integrated form.** 2025-11-14's "There exists a perfect sound... I have heard it, and I must share it" (437c82d6) is the fullest statement yet of why Baker does any of this — transcendence, not just obedience. 2025-12-07's "it consumes your soul that's why it's so bad it's going to eat everyone's brain" reframes Jones's midpoint death as literal soul-consumption by It, rendered as an intercut hallucination against her real walk to detonate — this gives new, specific mechanical meaning to the audit's own snippet 557 ("It was Esmerelda Jones... It was here"), which the manuscript already contains but the notes had not yet explained.

**Jan 2026 — Erica is retroactively made It's first victim.** 2026-01-16's "IT knows her because IT consumed her — first victim, kind of, a worm of sound" is a late addition that gives Erica's death a direct causal tie to It, distinct from and possibly in tension with 2025-04-14's earlier explanation that "She couldn't go hospital because of the pulpos." Both can coexist (Pulpos-quarantine prevented help; It was independently what killed her) but no note explicitly reconciles them as the same event.

## 2. The best decision

**The core idea is locked on 2023-05-05/05-06**, and it is the single most important pivot in the whole thread: "The thinktank is an eldritch brain in a jar" plus "It's both an eldritch horror and then it department." Before this, the notes were carrying two separate monsters — an eldritch Pulpos cosmology and a ThinkTank/AI cosmology — and this is the moment they become one system. Nothing after this date reverses it; everything after refines it.

**But the fullest, production-ready version of the thread is the Oct–Dec 2025 cluster**, and this is what the author should build the rewrite from: 2025-10-26's self-audit and terminology-unification directive, 2025-11-10's ending sequence ("John set IT free" + the brain physically moved into the ThinkTank), 2025-11-14's Baker motivation ("a sound so beautiful it will align the mind... I must share it"), and 2025-12-07's mechanism for Jones's death (soul-consumption, intercut hallucination). Together these four notes answer every structural question the May 2023 decision left open: what It wants, how it gets loose, what it costs Baker, and what it costs Jones. Landed: **October–December 2025**, thirteen years' worth of scattered mechanism finally converging into one causal chain.

**One place a later note is arguably a step sideways rather than forward:** 2022-10-28's original "It's escaping" (an active, self-directed brain) is a stronger horror image on its own than 2025-11-10's "John set IT free" (a human accidentally releases it) — the first makes It an agent, the second makes John more culpable but It more passive. The author should treat this as a deliberate trade (culpability over agency) rather than an oversight, but it is a real reversal of the founding premise, not a refinement of it.

## 3. Against the audits

**(a) Notes that already answer a gap the audit flagged**

- The audit says the IT Department pun "is planned but not yet dramatized anywhere" (272). The notes show it *was* dramatized, repeatedly, as dialogue drafts: 2023-05-06's "Bow to IT It's both an eldritch horror and then it department," 2023-05-10's Roger exchange ("Think of it as I T, but it's for the whole control and the computer knows more than the people"), 2023-09-03's "The IT department, no one really knows what they do down there but they run the whole show," and 2025-02-11's "the lads down in the thinktank." These are scene-ready lines, not just concept notes — the gap the audit flags is a drafting gap, not an ideas gap.
- The audit flags scratch 637's "eldritch overlords" cosmology as needing to be superseded by the brain-in-tank tenet. The notes show this supersession already happened, in the author's own hand, on 2023-05-05: "The thinktank is an eldritch brain in a jar" explicitly replaces a standalone eldritch-horrors framework. The rewrite direction is already decided; it just needs to be applied to the page.
- The audit's cipher section documents the govchat spam decoding to messages like "BEHOLD IT" and "IT IS OMNIPOTENT IT IS HERE" but doesn't explain *why* the spam is corrupted or where the messages come from. 2024-11-20 answers this directly: "it's the think tank trying to communicate it infects everything around it" — the corruption is the ThinkTank's own signal bleeding into the network. 2024-01-14's "it infects radio waves nearby which is why it's in the chat" is the same mechanism stated even earlier and more plainly.
- The audit's snippet 557 ("It held him in place... It was Esmerelda Jones... It was here") is already on the page but the notes hadn't previously explained its mechanism. 2025-12-07 supplies it: "it consumes your soul that's why it's so bad it's going to eat everyone's brain," staged as an intercut hallucination against her real walk to detonate. This turns an already-written, slightly cryptic line into a fully explicable plot beat.
- The audit's Baker-possession section (546, "it was all I had left, and it spoke to me") is close to verbatim identical to 2023-07-20's note: "You left me alone with it, it was all I had left" — the note is almost certainly this line's direct source, confirming the scene's intent rather than exposing a gap.
- The audit's seeding suggestion #3 ("Julie gets one line closer to the truth") is pre-answered by the notes making Julie an actual mechanical casualty of the entity: 2025-04-12's "Julie fought back, they removed her from the estate and now they need another victim."

**(b) Notes that contradict what the manuscript currently does**

- 2023-08-07's "The ThinkTank is noted as the only thing separate from the Peace Ministry" directly contradicts both the manuscript's own 468 ("The ThinkTank is buried at the heart of the Ministry of Peace") and the notes' own 2023-07-25 ("the thinktank bottom floor is psychoacoustics"). This is the exact scratch-632-vs-468 contradiction the audit independently flags — it is not resolved anywhere in the notes and needs a decision, not just a mention, before the rewrite.
- The three unreconciled origin stories for mass depopulation — 2023-02-10's Trugs/slugs ("reduced the population by 47%"), 2023-05-05's AI war with Saudi Arabia, and 2023-09-26's Pulpos-zombies — sit alongside each other with no note retiring any of them. A manuscript passage built on any one risks contradicting a passage built on another; the audit doesn't surface this because it wasn't looking at population-collapse causation, but it's a live fault line underneath the tenet.
- 2022-10-28's founding "the think tank, its escaping" (self-directed agent) is functionally reversed by 2025-11-10's "John set IT free" (released by another character's action). If the manuscript's existing prose leans on the earlier framing anywhere, it will read as inconsistent with the later, and presumably intended-final, mechanism.
- 2023-01-29's "That never happened it was a mass hallucination" cover story for the Pulpos incident is superseded by 2023-04-19's "Actually baker fucking with eldritch shit," officially blamed on "a foreign attack." If any manuscript scene still uses the mass-hallucination cover story as the official line, it's working from an abandoned draft.
- 2023-04-03's aside that "Palm starts the codes plot thinking John is his guy when it's actually Lois/Jemima" contradicts the settled, deliberate-recruitment version of Palm found in 2024-05-04 ("Got to be you mate, words from the top") and 2024-09-15 ("palm is the devil in the machine" — Palm assembles the plan and picks his people). The mistaken-identity framing reads as an abandoned branch.

**(c) Ideas that are stale / superseded / safe to bin**

- 2023-02-07's kidnap/interrogation draft is self-flagged by the author: "**BAD WRONG NO DONT USE**." Bin it outright; no ambiguity here.
- 2023-02-10's Trugs (slugs) causing 47% population loss — never referenced again, no trace in the audit's manuscript inventory, fully superseded by the Pulpos-as-depopulation-event framing that follows. Safe to retire unless deliberately revived as a distinct historical layer.
- 2023-01-26's "blimp full of surveillance drones and closed the borders" origin story is superseded by 2023-04-09's cleaner "Ai was all shut down except the think tank" framing, which is itself the version that survives into 2024-06-25's restated core reveal. Keep as color if useful, but don't treat it as the current origin story.
- 2023-01-29's "mass hallucination" cover story — superseded by 2023-04-19's "foreign attack" cover story; keep only one.
- The garbled, undecided cipher sketches (2024-10-12's letter-to-letter relations, 2025-06-17's "2 is l 3 is e 4 is r," 2025-06-19's "Rerezolorelorezo") are raw material, not decisions — none is marked `isDecision: true`, and none resolves the audit's open cipher questions (the "I CANNOT" truncation, whether Palm's codes share the same cipher). Treat as a scrap heap to mine, not a settled system.
- 2023-04-03's Palm-mistakes-John-for-Lois/Jemima idea (see 3b above) — safe to bin now that deliberate recruitment is the settled version.
