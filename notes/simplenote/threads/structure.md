# Thread: Structure

## 1. Evolution

**Oct–Nov 2022 — triage mode: cut, don't yet redesign.** The earliest notes are all knife-work on an existing draft: "Do harmony better" (2022-10-27), "Bollocks get rid" (2022-10-27, `isDecision: true`), and the to-do list "Fix Julie / Fix palm / Shorten" (2022-10-27, `isDecision: true`) for the opening 120 pages. The one real structural move in this batch is the midpoint restructure of 2022-10-28: "merge the pub meeting with Cesira, cut a redundant Roger/John scene, and have Palm hand John the codes as Jones dies, dragging him into the plot" (keyQuote "YOUR POCKET MR BLARD," `isDecision: true`) — the first version of the handoff-at-death beat that survives, reshaped, for four years. The same week locks a constraint that never gets reversed: **"He can't go home. It has to happen while he's with them."** (2022-10-28, `isDecision: true`).

**Nov 2022 — cutting a whole section, then debating the worldbuilding under it.** "Remove basically whole second section and cut to travel" (2022-11-16, `isDecision: true`) is a major excision, immediately followed by open questions the cut created — a time-gap plot hole, and whether the Tumult are "plumbers" or "just terrorists" (2022-11-16, not yet decided). The crows/Big Ben set-piece is also cut in favour of "drones killing loads of people, quelling an uprising as vetko comes back" (2022-11-24, `isDecision: true`) — a decision later revisited (see 2026-06-18 below).

**2023 — the long middle: mystery-withholding, pacing swings, and a false start on the opening.** 2023-02-08 makes the thread's first real reader-psychology decision: **"Is it better if you don't know what happened to Jones? Yep."** (`isDecision: true`) — not knowing is what re-engages John. 2023-04-21 locks the mechanical spine still visible in the finished scene-map — John without codes, Palm's warning, flight, Reza's retrieval, "Jones blow up," then the codes (keyQuote "Jones blow up," `isDecision: true`). But 2023-07-13's **"Start with Jones death?"** (`isDecision: true`) — a flash-forward opening floated for pace — is never mentioned again and is not what the book does; the scene-map's Chapter 1 is the Watch meeting, in chronological order. Likewise 2023-01-09's candidate opening line, "England 2050. Nothing much has changed." (`isDecision: true`), leaves no trace in the scene-map's actual cold open (Session 84 convening as mock ritual). Both read as abandoned drafts of the opening, not the version that shipped. 2023-07-11 is a pile-up of same-day notes, one of them genuinely load-bearing: **"John is the main character, I guess I wanted that not to be the case though"** — a tension the author sits with for two and a half years before resolving it (see 2026-01-24).

**2024 — the midpoint gets its final, explicit name.** After two years of circling it, 2024-04-29 states the whole book's shape in one line: **"ALL JONES, THEN SHE DIES THEN ALL JOHN"** (`isDecision: true`). This is the single sentence the rest of the thread is organized around, and it matches the scene-map precisely — Jones dominates snippets 1–256, dies in "The Cloudtop Bombing" (251–256), and the book is John's from there. The same year sharpens the information control around her death: "Don't see the second half of her being caught just the first" (2024-09-18, `isDecision: true`) — refining, not reversing, the 2023-02-08 mystery decision, and confirmed on the page (191–198 shows the capture; 413–418 shows the rest, later, as recovered tape). 2024-04-28's "Cut the station just have them come out in the cake shop" (`isDecision: true`) is a small merge decision that is directly visible in the finished scene-map ("364-368 — Crisis in the cake shop backroom").

**2025 — a revision pass that both fixes and re-breaks continuity.** 2025-06-22 is the thread's most consequential single note: a punch-list to "cut excessive Family-Guy-style pub jokes, keep the Reza-and-John/John-not-going-home material working, cut the pointless character Daz, and refine Jones's death scene so she looks at Roger and the big moment is whispered rather than stated" (`isDecision: true`). The death-scene fix ("whisper it not say it") sticks. **The cut of Daz does not** — Daz is still an active Govchat regular through the finished scene-map (62-63, 143-145, 167-168, 208-209) — a decision made and apparently not carried out. 2025-04-12's ironic chapter-naming scheme ("Esmerelda jones holds the key," "Roger Harvey saves the world," `isDecision: true`) is itself reversed eight months later by a different, plainer title list (2025-12-30: Descent, Anticitizen John, Mom's the Word...) that matches actual scene-map headings ("256-261 — Anti Citizen John," "89-93... 'MoM's the word'") — the later scheme is the one that shipped. Late in the year, 2025-10-26 is the thread's other pivotal note — see §3(a) — and 2025-11-24's decision to cut Jemima, folding her into Cesira "to double Cesira's narrative weight" (`isDecision: true`), is contradicted by the scene-map: Jemima remains a distinct, active character throughout (341-346, 429-435, 538-541).

**Jan 2026 — the doctrine that finally explains why the 2024 handover works.** The three extensive 2026-01-24 notes are the thread's culmination, not a new plan: **"the world keeps changing around him"**; Jones's death is "bureaucratic, not heroic," Palm a "meta-custodian not a saviour"; and the rule that **"the midpoint shift must never be softened, explained, or comforted afterward."** This retroactively resolves 2023-07-11's discomfort with John being "the main character" — the doctrine reframes him as "the narrative carrier not the emotional centre," which is a structural answer to a feeling the author had held unresolved for 30 months. It also confirms the ending shape later visible in the scene-map (drones down and Harmony gone don't equal resolution; Block/Vetko escapes — matching 606-612's helicopter exit).

**2026 (through June) — late course-corrections that outrun the current draft.** 2026-06-18 insists dropped threads "must matter and not just vanish" — walking back some of the 2022-11-24 Big Ben cut, and matching the scene-map's actual payoff (Bigger Ben is blown up at 436-440; crow imagery recurs throughout) — a good sign the correction landed. But the same day's other decision, that the book "ends at the gardens of Chatsworth" styled after the "bachelor" Sixth Duke of Devonshire, **does not match** the scene-map's actual final scenes (623, "The Actual End: to the pub," followed by a skull-mountain dream). This is the most recent note in the whole thread and it is currently ahead of, not reflected in, the draft — either a planned rewrite of the ending or an idea not yet executed.

## 2. The best decision

**2024-04-29's "ALL JONES, THEN SHE DIES THEN ALL JOHN"** is the strongest structural version of this book, and it is decisively the one to build from. It ends two years of wobbling over where to open (flash-forward vs. chronological), what to withhold, and whose book this is — by simply naming the shape: Jones owns the first half, her death is the hinge, John owns the second half. The scene-map confirms it shipped almost exactly as written.

What makes it *the* best decision, rather than just *a* decision, is that the 2026-01-24 doctrine subsequently explains why it has to stay hard-edged: the midpoint "must never be softened, explained, or comforted afterward," and Jones's death must read as "bureaucratic, not heroic." Read together, these two notes — eighteen months apart — form one complete, load-bearing structural rule: a clean chronological handover, at a death that is deliberately withheld from being made meaningful in the moment. Every other structural idea in the thread (the flash-forward opening, the ironic chapter titles, the Chatsworth ending) is either a discarded alternative to this spine or a still-unresolved edge case around it — this is the spine itself.

- **2022-10-27's very first note on this thread — "don't repeat beats" — is the same complaint, four years earlier.** That it recurs as a fresh, urgent note in 2025-10-26 shows the repetition problem was flagged early, "fixed" in spirit repeatedly, and kept coming back — worth noting as the thread's one true chronic issue, not a one-time slip.

## 4. Worth holding

- **The 2026-01-24 doctrine notes**, in full — "John does not change the world. The world keeps changing around him," "Jones's death is bureaucratic, not heroic," "the midpoint shift must never be softened, explained, or comforted afterward." This is the governing structural law for the whole book and should be the first thing any editor of this thread re-reads.
- **2025-10-26's "vary the repetitive chapter structure and the repeated drone fight"** — an instruction the author already gave themself that maps almost one-to-one onto the repetition-report's findings; execute it directly against that report's specific groups rather than re-diagnosing the problem again.
- **2026-06-18's "dropped threads... must matter and not just vanish"** — a good standing principle, already validated by Big Ben's and the crows' real payoffs on the page; worth applying to any other thread the author is tempted to abandon.
- **2025-09-18's chapter-opening Vetko epigraphs** — not yet visible anywhere in the scene-map, but a cheap, high-value way to build Vetko ("I have a strong belief in the transience of human nature. They'll believe whatever you tell them.") without adding scenes.
- **2024-02-04's "he should realize over book one what's happening — or have hints and vetko tells him later"** — the clearest statement of John's slow-burn arc; worth keeping explicit as later drafts compress or expand his POV.
- **2023-05-15's sequel structure ("It's told as John watches Erica die. The tumult happens around them as she's dying.")** — not this book's structure, but should be preserved as the deliberate bookend to Jones's death here; the two deaths are clearly meant to rhyme across the series.
