# Thread: John Hiding, Not Scared

## 1. Evolution

**2022-10 to 2022-11: hiding starts as pure psychology, not fugitive craft.** The earliest notes explain John's withdrawal entirely in emotional terms — grief, aging, self-loathing — with no concealment mechanic attached. "*And so he stayed inside as the world fell apart, an unstable society shrinking closer and closer to his little bit of security*" (47acab9c, 2022-10-27) is a retreat metaphor, not tradecraft. Jones names it as resignation, not danger-avoidance: "*The world got so scary you turned away from it, but that's just getting old that is*" (d4658aeb, 2022-11-09). "*Dreams are ridiculous. Give up on your dreams*" (25747d18, 2022-11-14) and "*I came out of my shell once when I was 23, turns out nobody liked me, including myself. Its better in here*" (b5ff62b6, 2022-11-24) both frame his shell as social self-protection, not concealment from the state. This is exactly the "grief wrapped in anxiety comedy" reading the audit says now dominates the manuscript through roughly snippet 250 — these notes are its origin, not a counterweight to it.

On 2022-10-28 the author separately locks a structural constraint that has nothing to do with fear vs. hiding as psychology: "*He can't go home. It has to happen while he's with them*" (b6f03e55, decision) — John's exile must occur while trapped with the Tumult, ruling out a solo choice to flee. A same-day aside, "*He can be crazy and trapped with the tum tums*" (e8f40c0a), is a weaker, non-decision variant of the same idea and is superseded within hours by b6f03e55.

**2023-04 to 2023-05: the fugitive mechanic finally appears.** This is the real turn. Structural notes establish that John becomes "*a wanted man*" who has to run (c824cffa, 2023-04-06), gets the alias "*john bomb*" (0c14b871, 2023-04-15), and — the strongest single beat in the whole thread — "*There are no pictures of John Blard. Just one of Erica*," with John masking whenever a drone is present (07d685f8, 2023-04-15, decision). For the first time, hiding is staged as a literal, physical practice rather than a mood. Weeks later, "*So john Blard put down the mask. And Doug Goode picked up the gun*" (513170c7, 2023-05-15, decision) gives that concealment a name and a past identity to protect.

But the very same window undercuts itself. "*Man what are you running away from, is it death or is it life?*" (7b18b18a, 2023-05-02, decision) keeps the question open between mortality-dread and hiding. Worse, two weeks after inventing the mask, the author writes John saying the quiet part out loud: "*Because the only thing I have left now is the fact that I'm alive and I'm scared Reza*" (d0a5d482, 2023-05-30). That is John using the word "scared," in his own dialogue, about being alive — the single note in the whole set that most directly contradicts the tenet, arriving right after the mask/alter-ego mechanic was supposed to settle it.

**2023-09 to 2023-10: hiding gets a reason instead of a mood.** Small tells accumulate — John "*knows stuff about the background he shouldnt — drones can't hear music*" (a067feef, 2023-09-07) and met Jones over an illegal mixtape (9536faed, 2023-09-26) — texture for a man with a hidden life. Then the decisive reframe: "*She knows none of it is real*" ... Doug "also knows and that's why he hides, per what she told him" (70237792, 2023-10-29, decision). This is the moment hiding stops being explained by fear or grief and becomes explained by knowledge — he conceals himself because of what he knows, not because he's afraid of what might happen to him.

**2024-06 to 2024-11: the secret gets concrete, and a "why him" answer appears.** 8a1901fd (2024-06-01) grounds John's pre-hiding life as an ordinary government slogan-writer — useful ballast against the "sharper spy-like character" temptation the author flags later. Then a2039f9e (2024-11-10, decision) makes the 70237792 idea usable: "*no one knows she died, she disappeared and started a terror group, only John and vetko know the truth*." This gives John a specific, dangerous, exclusive piece of knowledge — exactly the kind of thing that would make a state actor seek him out by name. Three weeks later, 55dadf88 (2024-11-29, decision) defines the actual growth beat, independent of the fear/hiding question: "*That's the growth — he sees this, hates it and tries to run away*," when Reza is hurt and John goes back for him anyway.

**2025-02 to 2025-04: the deflection gets content, and the author names the tenet outright.** b5acca29 (2025-02-10, decision) — "*John knows jones is going to confront the mast people*" — gives John's evasiveness in that scene a concrete thing he's withholding, not just anxiety. Then 395f5071 (2025-04-12) states the whole thread in one line, in the author's own voice: "*He's in marketing and he's hiding*," while explicitly weighing "how he thinks/speaks versus a sharper spy-like character" — proof the author was already aware of the tension between an ordinary-guy voice and tradecraft competence that the audit later flags as a "retcon feeling."

**2026-01: a late relapse, then a governing doctrine.** f3295d76 (2026-01-06) stages the first Reza meeting with John "*shitting self*" in front of someone "huge, armed, terrifying" — played straight, comic, with no accompanying hiding-tell. Coming this late, it risks reinforcing the exact "comic coward" surface the audit says currently owns the first quarter of the book. Eighteen days later, the two long doctrine entries (3f5e8ef4, 9259906e, both 2026-01-24, decisions) supersede everything above at the structural level: John is "*the narrative carrier not the emotional centre*," "*John does not change the world. The world keeps changing around him*," and — the line that directly governs this thread — his real past, "Doug Goode, Raven," "*belongs only in the prequel*." That last clause retroactively restricts how much of the 2023 alter-ego material (513170c7, 70237792, a2039f9e) can ever be spelled out on the page of this book; it must stay implied, which actually agrees with the audit's own preferred fix (tells, not exposition). But "the world keeps changing around him" also risks license for more of the passivity the audit already diagnoses as a problem ("I've done my bit," 440/487-488) — it sits in real tension with "his refusals are camouflage discipline," which requires John to be an active, willing concealer, not just a passenger.

## 2. The best decision

The strongest version of this thread is the compound decision that **John hides because he holds a specific, dangerous secret — not because he is afraid of danger, and not primarily because of grief.** It starts with the mask mechanic in spring 2023 (07d685f8, 0c14b871, 513170c7), is named explicitly on **2023-10-29** ("*that's why he hides, per what she told him*," 70237792), and is made concrete and usable on **2024-11-10** ("*only John and vetko know the truth*," a2039f9e). That last note is the one to build from: it is specific enough to write scenes against (Palm wants him for a reason; his deflections have content), and it directly answers the audit's biggest structural gap (see below).

The 2026-01-24 doctrine (3f5e8ef4/9259906e) should govern *how* this gets written — obliquely, prequel-locked — but it does not replace the 2024-11-10 decision; it constrains its delivery. And the two moments of drift (d0a5d482's "I'm scared Reza" in 2023-05, and f3295d76's played-straight fear beat in 2026-01) are not better ideas that should stand — they're relapses into the "scared" surface the whole rest of the thread was built to get past, and the "best decision" reading should treat them as material to reframe, not follow.

## 3. Against the audits

**(a) Notes that already answer a gap the audit flagged.**
- The audit's single largest gap: *"John never once wonders why him. He treats Palm as random weather... The gap: one beat of John suspecting Palm knows who he is would convert the whole Palm thread from absurdist harassment into dread."* **a2039f9e (2024-11-10)** — "*no one knows she died, she disappeared and started a terror group, only John and vetko know the truth*" — is the missing reason. It gives John, and potentially Palm, an actual motive for the state's fixation on him that has nothing to do with luck. This is ready to be dropped into the Palm thread as John's private, unstated suspicion.
- Hardening suggestion #5 wants Jones's "*Whats so special about you?*" to land as a question John visibly declines to answer, so it primes Doris's "man on the run" reveal. **b5acca29 (2025-02-10)** — "*John knows jones is going to confront the mast people*" — supplies exactly the concrete, withheld content for that deflection: he isn't evading because he's scared of the question, he's evading because he already knows the answer.
- The audit flags the charisma/luck payoffs (Town Hall speech, Reza's "nine lives") as unseeded and retcon-feeling. **55dadf88 (2024-11-29)** — "*That's the growth — he sees this, hates it and tries to run away*" (John returns for an injured Reza) — is a concrete, in-notes growth beat that could anchor those later payoffs in an earned action rather than unexplained luck.

**(b) Notes that contradict what the manuscript currently does.**
- **d0a5d482 (2023-05-30)**: "*I'm scared Reza*" — John saying this in his own dialogue is the most direct possible contradiction of "not scared." If any version of this line is in the current draft unpaired with a hiding-tell, it is actively reinforcing the audit's "fear as stated refusal logic" reading (177, 221, 243) rather than undercutting it.
- **f3295d76 (2026-01-06)**: the first Reza meeting, John "*shitting self*," played straight for comedy — this is a late note that still writes fear as the punchline with no counterweight, at a first-impression beat that will anchor the reader's read of the relationship. It sits on the wrong side of the tenet unless paired with a tradecraft tell in the same scene.
- **9259906e (2026-01-24)**: "*John does not change the world. The world keeps changing around him*" is in tension with the tenet's own framing — "*His refusals are camouflage discipline, not cowardice*" requires an active, willing concealer. A pure narrative-carrier/passenger framing reads closer to the "inertia/routine" diagnosis the audit already flags (153-154, 440, 487-488) than to deliberate tradecraft. This doesn't have to be a contradiction — discipline can be quiet — but as written it gives cover for more passivity, not less, and should be watched.

**(c) Stale / superseded / safe to bin.**
- The whole run of 2022 psychological-only notes — 47acab9c (2022-10-27, "shrinking closer to his little bit of security"), 5b72038d (2022-11-02), d4658aeb (2022-11-09, "that's just getting old"), 25747d18 (2022-11-14, "give up on your dreams"), b5ff62b6 (2022-11-24, "nobody liked me including myself") — are already fully and successfully realized in the manuscript's dominant grief/aging read, per the audit's section 1. They don't need more attention; more material in this vein deepens the exact problem (grief crowding out hiding) rather than fixing it.
- **e8f40c0a (2022-10-28)**, "he can be crazy and trapped with the tum tums," is a same-day, non-decision variant superseded by **b6f03e55**'s firmer decision.
- **c4c12efe / 2cfdaff6 (2023-11-16)**, open plot-hole worries about why John never goes home after Cesira, read as resolved by the later concrete outline in **c824cffa (2023-04-06)** and **9bdc103e (2023-04-21)** — safe to retire as standing questions.
- **8a1901fd (2024-06-01)**, the government-slogan-writer backstory, is fine background but doesn't move the hiding-vs-scared needle either way; no further revision needed there.
