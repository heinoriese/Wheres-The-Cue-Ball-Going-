# Thread: Other Characters (Baker, Roger, Doris, Cesira, Delta, Maurice, Ethel, Stanley, Wozzy, Paul Bond, minor cast)

Synthesis of 196 notes (27 Oct 2022 – 18 Jun 2026) covering the secondary/minor cast not already handled by the Jones, John, Vetko, Reza, or Palm threads. Organised by character. Dates in parentheses are note dates, not manuscript snippets.

## Ian Baker ("The Snake", Minister of Peace)

**Evolution.** Baker starts as flavour — a "loges it" fragment (31 Oct 2022) and a chauvinist aside ("woman weren't sexually activated at first, we did that to you," 18 Dec 2022) that reads uncomfortably now but was clearly meant to mark him as inhuman/possessed rather than a real seduction beat. Through 2023 he accretes texture rather than plot: a "cruel optimism" theory of self-improvement-as-control ("The stupid ones railed against it, the smart ones turned it against them," 6 Jan 2023), the running gag that he treats science as music and hums constantly "to tune his brain to operate at maximum capacity" (6 Mar 2023, `isDecision`), and a de-energised, inhuman affect ("He thinks blinking is a waste of time," 17 May 2023; "De energized dreamy and far away," 14 Jul 2023). The turn comes across 2024: a run of decisions systematically demotes him from mastermind to puppet. He isn't in charge — "Baker isn't bothered, Felicity basically makes the decisions" (6 Oct 2024, `isDecision`) and "He gets orders from the think tank through in his office / Democracy is a simulacrum... of something that no longer exists" (25 Nov 2024, `isDecision`). By 2025 the notes finally give him an actual desire rather than just a function: he is obsessed with psychoacoustics and believes he has heard "a sound so beautiful it will align the mind, enter it into a flow state... And I have heard it, and I must share it" (14 Nov 2025, `isDecision`) — a real villain motive, arriving three years into the drafting.

**Best/latest decision.** The 14 Nov 2025 psychoacoustics speech is the strongest and most recent characterisation note — it's the one place Baker gets a coherent want (weaponised transcendence) rather than just a set of tics and a subordinate role. Paired with "BAKER TAKES INSTRUCTIONS FROM IT ALL THE WAY THROUGH; it guides him" (7 Nov 2025, `isDecision`), it completes his arc: a man chasing a sound he thinks is enlightenment, used the whole time by the thing making the sound.

**Gap/contradiction vs. manuscript.** The notes contain **three different ideas for how Baker dies**, never reconciled: (1) 13 Feb 2025 — "Vetko brainwashes Baker with the IT/Harmony entity, Baker sets it free of course he does and it eats him" (`isDecision`); (2) 6 Jun 2025 — Vetko shoots him: "Because you don't matter Ian. Nobody matters but me" (`isDecision`); (3) the manuscript audit records him "knifed 596" in the finale, apparently by Wozzy. None of eaten/shot/knifed is flagged in the notes as superseding the others — worth deciding on the page which death survives, since all three carry strong dialogue.

## Roger Harvey

**Evolution.** Roger begins as Watch-chairman furniture who nearly arrests John ("He runs. Roger sees him, tries to arrest? elleray saves," 29 Oct 2022) and "only goes mental once" in meeting revisions (28 Oct 2022). He gets real backstory in 2023: a special constable because the state can't afford real police (24 Jan 2023), a Tumult veteran "injured during the tumult... that's why he's retired and doing his best" (8 Apr 2023, `isDecision`), a verbal tic ("Listen \" the perfect beginning to any sentence \"," 12 Apr 2023), and is set up as "OPP site John so wants to make amends" (12 Apr 2023). The darkest fork in his arc is 1 May 2023: "He has Roger kill Julie / Vetko thinks it awesome, so leaves baker there and takes roger" (`isDecision`) — Roger as Vetko's chosen instrument, not Baker. A serious, underused thread appears 30 Jun 2024: Roger "wrestles with internalized prejudice against" an immigrant character, remembering "a time when natural kindness was expected" and keeping her close "despite physically straining against his own bias" — labelled by the author as "Backwards racism, trying to capture your own wrestle with knowing it's wrong." This beat does not appear anywhere in the manuscript audit's Roger coverage (Watch chairman → Party celebrity/Inquisitor → harmonised bodyguard) and may have been dropped or never drafted.

**Best/latest decision.** The most recent Roger-specific instruction is in the 26 Oct 2025 punch-list: "cut Roger's escape-by-bus and activate him earlier since he's a 'party man' who'd go quietly" — a late simplification of his harmonisation, making him complicit rather than resistant, which tracks better with the audit's read of his arc (chairman → celebrity → conditioned bodyguard) than the earlier "wants to make amends" framing does.

**Gap/contradiction.** The 1 May 2023 "Roger kills Julie" decision directly conflicts with the later worldbuilding (12 Apr 2025, see Stanley below) where Julie "fought back" and was simply "removed from the estate" rather than murdered. If the later mechanism is what's on the page, Roger's hands are clean of Julie's death and the "shame" the audit notes as an unresolved echo at 457 needs a different source — or the note needs to be read as an early, discarded idea.

## Rosemary Doris

**Evolution.** Thin but atmospheric. Two sensory-only notes: her shop stocked with "realist party merchandise" (26 Mar 2025) and smelling of patchouli (5 Apr 2025). The character's plot function comes from an earlier note not tagged with her name directly but naming her: Julie "reached them [the tumtums] via Rosemary Doris" (7 Feb 2023, `isDecision`) — she's the connective node between Julie and the resistance, not just set dressing. A much later note (30 May 2026) has John and Reza trade a coded exchange "before going in to see Doris," implying her shop doubles as a rendezvous point for signalling the others about "that night."

**Gap.** The manuscript audit lists her as "Psychic; gateway to Nimbysville" (326–338, 471–477) — consistent with the connective-node function implied by the 2023 note, but the notes never actually develop what she says or does as a psychic; only atmosphere and one plot-function line exist.

## Cesira Brogue

**Evolution.** Introduced structurally ("merge the pub meeting with Cesira," 28 Oct 2022) and confirmed to work as written ("Cesira stuffs alright actually," 16 Nov 2022). The single most important decision for her arrives startlingly early — 16 Jan 2023: "Cesira and 'Jem' are the same person... she was never evil, just bad at her job" (`isDecision`) — nearly three years before it's executed. Backstory follows: a Truss-like ex-leader of the opposition, "idealistic, shit" (11 Feb 2023), with a specific gag number, "112-hour premiership" (3 Apr 2023). Her core theme crystallises 29 Sep 2024: she "wants desperately to matter and be the main character," ordering others out so she can take "the important place" she never actually gets — "A regal queen awaiting c[or]on[a]tion as the main character / Back to where she deserves to be" (`isDecision`). The merge with Jemima is finally executed 24 Nov 2025: "Of jemima / Pour her into cesira" (`isDecision`), "doubling Cesira's narrative weight" per the note's own framing.

**Best/latest decision.** 2 Jan 2026 gives her death, and it closes the "wants to matter" theme perfectly: her rage "crystallises into a demand to matter," and Vetko dismisses it before killing her — "'That's beautiful' Vetko said 'but what are you going to do with it?' and he crushed it, and she stumbled, and then she was falling" (`isDecision`). This **matches the manuscript audit's account of her death** (thrown from the roof, snippet 610) — a rare case of a late note and the finished page agreeing exactly.

**Note answering a gap.** The 16 Jan 2023 Cesira/Jemima merge decision, sitting two years before its 2025 execution, is useful evidence that the "cut Jemima" call in the audit's minor-character list is a long-held instinct finally acted on, not a last-minute cut.

## Delta Isol

**Evolution.** Thin material, both notes about fear rather than character depth: she reacts to crows outside — "Crows everywhere delt" (11 May 2023), explaining why Reza and John go alone — and later whispers underground "for fear the Pulpos will come" (10 Feb 2024). No arc, no backstory; the manuscript audit's fuller Delta thread (car-bomb detonation, spine broken at 608) has no seed at all in this notes thread — her physical-stakes material was evidently developed later or elsewhere.

## Maurice Doris

**Evolution.** Maurice's best material is his earliest: a confession scene (17 May 2023, `isDecision`) admitting he voted for the regime, framed by "The pub on the village green was turned into a wether spoons decades ago, we just couldn't accept it" — a genuinely rich complicity/self-deception beat. He then drifts toward comic relief: a wandering-eye physical gag (15 Jan 2025 — "one eye that went like this and the other eye that went like that"), a group-rescue beat flagged as needing more staging thought ("Maurice saves them all at once," 26 Nov 2024), and his best quotable line, comparing someone to "a fossilized apex predator. Extinct, but still perfectly evolved for the ecosystem that used to exist" (3 Nov 2025). The 5 Nov 2025 action-beat note has him arriving to complete the rescue.

**Best/latest decision.** The 3 Nov 2025 "fossilized apex predator" line is the strongest quotable material for him and the most recent addition — worth checking it survived into the actual rescue scene, since the note that follows it (5 Nov 2025) is pure blocking, not dialogue.

## Ethel Cobbler (planted early as Esme)

**Evolution.** Small but load-bearing set of notes. Two are pure comic texture — unknowingly farting in the van (19 Jul 2023: "she doesn't know she's doing it or she doesn't care") and being "the nannest nan that ever nanned," desperate to see her grandkids (19 Aug 2023). The functional one is 10 Feb 2024: a checkpoint scene where a character is flagged suspicious and "ethel rescues him, ellwray chases him down to also save him" (`isDecision`) — this is the Esme/Ethel plant-and-payoff the manuscript audit specifically singles out as "the model the author could reuse for Palm." A later note (24 Apr 2025) confirms the Esme identity and adds a payoff line: "esme has to call him John bomb" in the "fat goat" scene.

**Gap.** Given how well this small thread lines up with the audit's own praise of the Esme/Ethel technique, it's a candidate worth protecting rather than trimming in any pass that's cutting minor-character material.

## Stanley

**Evolution.** Only two notes, but the second is one of the most important finds in this whole thread. 10 May 2023 first frames "Stanley's control over everything" as an IT metaphor: "Think of it as I T, but it's for the whole control and the computer knows more than the people" (`isDecision`). The major note is 12 Apr 2025 (`isDecision`): Stanley is "an ordinary, friendly IT technician also under the entity's influence; it wants to cross into human minds via sound but sometimes fries people, and **Julie fought back, they removed her from the estate and now they need another victim**."

**Answers a gap.** This note directly resolves the open question the manuscript audit flags about Julie's fate after snippet 235 ("never resolved on-page"): Julie is not killed here, she fights the entity off and is simply removed, and the plot then needs a replacement victim — almost certainly Roger, whose harmonisation the audit records at snippet 463. It also **supersedes** the 1 May 2023 "Roger kills Julie" decision (see Roger, above) — the two cannot both be true as written.

## Wozzy (Zone Drone 063)

**Evolution.** Just two notes, both plot-mechanical rather than characterful. 15 Apr 2023 (`isDecision`) clarifies the drone hierarchy: "Evan is the Mark 3 drone controller; Wozzy was the only one, palm didn't know it was moving" — a specific plot-logic point (Palm's ignorance of Wozzy's autonomy) worth confirming is preserved, since it underwrites Wozzy's later independent role as ally/weapon. 14 Apr 2025 (`isDecision`) is a cut: "skip" a planned Jones-and-Wozzy scene; "she instead just turns up at John's next." A small but real streamlining choice.

## Paul Bond

**Evolution.** Bond is built almost entirely as a pathetic-comic figure rather than a plot function. Early on he's collateral fallout from Julie's bat ("paul bond is not happy about it," 27 Oct 2022). His defining backstory note is 25 Jan 2023: he "wanted to be a writer, ended up an online comment-section pundit" chasing "a sense of intellectual superiority quite similar to actually achieving something" — a sharp, very specific comic-tragic sketch. 8 Feb 2023 gives him a mock-earnest bad poem ending "Cause chaos!!!!", and 10 May 2023 has him threatening Roger over illegally shared utilities, with "Roger quotes law allowing him to do it" — regime-bureaucracy comedy at his expense.

**Gap.** The manuscript audit records him dying in the Watch bombing (snippet 253) with only his family mentioned afterward — none of this richer characterisation (frustrated-writer backstory, the poem) is visible in the audit's terse appearance list. Worth checking whether the "intellectual superiority" backstory and the poem made it to the page before his death, since they're his best material and currently exist only as notes.

## Felicity Scrope / Carter (tied tightly to Baker's arc)

Not one of the ten named leads but recurring enough to flag. The manuscript audit notes a name inconsistency, "Felicity Scrope / Felicity Carter," flagged at snippets 506–507 — and the notes explain exactly why: 17 Apr 2023 records an explicit but apparently unexecuted rename decision, "Victoria Scrope - change Felicity Carter to something Scrope" (`isDecision`). The rename evidently only half-landed (Scrope stuck, Victoria didn't, and Carter lingers in places). Otherwise she's established as the real power behind Baker — "Felicity basically makes the decisions" (6 Oct 2024) — and as the one who tells him about Jones: "Felicity tells him the crows done it" (2 Jun 2024, `isDecision`).

- **Naming sparks** (Snow, Raine, 4 Oct 2023; Japanese-but-French-accent quirk, 24 Mar 2024; "Alan's cabal of cheese men... Indiana Jones but it's in Buxton," 27 Apr 2025) — unassigned ideas, likely unused; flagged here in case any were meant for named minor characters.

- **2 Jan 2026 — Cesira's death: "That's beautiful... and he crushed it, and she stumbled, and then she was falling."** Confirmed against the manuscript (thrown from the roof, 610) — a rare note-to-page match, and the cleanest payoff of her "wants to matter" throughline.

- **16 Jan 2023 — "Cesira and 'Jem' are the same person... she was never evil, just bad at her job."** A two-years-early instinct that was finally executed (24 Nov 2025); good evidence of a consistent authorial through-line worth trusting elsewhere.

- **17 May 2023 — Maurice's Wetherspoons confession.** The single richest thematic line for any minor character in the thread; worth confirming it's still in the manuscript given Maurice's later drift toward comic relief.
- **10 Feb 2024 — Ethel's checkpoint rescue.** The plant-and-payoff the audit itself praises as a reusable technique — protect this from any minor-character trim.
- **29 Oct 2022 / 22 Jun 2025 — Daz created, then cut.** Flag for verification: the audit's Watch-members list still includes him post-bombing, but the author explicitly cut him.
