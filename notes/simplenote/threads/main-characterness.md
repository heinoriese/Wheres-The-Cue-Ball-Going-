# Thread: Main-Characterness

## 1. Evolution

**Oct–Nov 2022 — it starts as Palm's weirdness, not John's power.** The earliest notes (2022-10-27, three of them) aren't about John having a metaphysical property at all — they're about Elleray Palm going "weird" only when alone with John ("Oh no no please don't go all weird, I mean weirder"), addressing him with uncanny familiarity ("Way way weirder and winky and eh blard you'll be there right mate"), and pressuring him into something. 2022-10-28's "crazy Blard" scene locks the shape that recurs for years: John tries to run, Palm drags him back, and the line that will become the tenet's engine appears in embryo — "you were done the second you met the fat man." At this stage the "power" reads as Palm's plot function (recruit John, don't take no for an answer), not yet as John radiating anything himself.

**2022-11-28 — the founding meta-conceit.** This is the pivot the whole thread hangs from: "Vetko wrote the world. He is God, losing control of his own world, he's meant to be the main character, but it's her." This is the first note to state, as a decision, that main-characterness is a *contested, transferable* property rather than a fixed trait — and that Vetko's whole arc is the trauma of losing it. 2022-12-01 immediately extends the mechanism with a narrator-voice device: "Voice at first is him looking / Voice after is her" — the earliest technical idea for how the property could be felt on the page as a formal shift, not just stated.

**Jan 2023 — the transfer gets a literal, hideable mechanism.** 2023-01-20 is a hard decision, not a spark: "He has the kill code in his head because he and his wife had it implanted in them, elleray palm knows this, nobody else does, Erica also had it." This is the first time the "passed from Erica to John" idea in the tenet gets an actual physical object (a shared implant) rather than remaining poetic. 2023-02-07 gives Palm a mechanical *plot* function to match his mythic one: he "forces John into" the Julie investigation by lying to the tumtums about what John's doing — i.e., Palm manufactures John's relevance rather than merely detecting it, a tension the notes never fully resolve (see Section 3b).

**Spring 2023 — two competing corrections, both marked decisions.** 2023-04-03 complicates the "Palm always correctly recognizes John" reading that the tenet assumes: "Palm starts the codes plot thinking John is his guy when it's actually Lois/Jemima." This is a real wrinkle — if Palm's original target was a mistaken identity, the "recognition liturgy" the manuscript audit prizes (Palm's uncanny certainty) may have started as an *error*, not detection. One month later, 2023-05-02 pulls hard in the opposite direction, reasserting Jones against John's creeping centrality: "Jones has to remain important because it's her that drives the start of the book" — an explicit anti-drift note, the author catching the manuscript giving John too much gravity too early.

**April–May 2023 — the identity apparatus (the thread's second major cluster).** 2023-04-15 is the load-bearing decision for how the property stays hidden: "There are no pictures of John Blard. Just one of Erica." — John is masked whenever a drone is present, known publicly only as a composite sketch. One month later, 2023-05-15 escalates this from concealment into full alter-ego: "So john Blard put down the mask. / And Doug Goode picked up the gun," with the Raven "invoking the name Doug Goode constantly." Together these three notes (kill code, no-photo mask, Doug Goode) are the *entire* mechanical toolkit the tenet audit is looking for and not finding on the page — see Section 3a.

**July 2023 — the author names the tension directly.** 2023-07-11 is not tagged as a decision, but it's the single most self-aware line in the whole thread: "John is the main character, I guess I wanted that not to be the case though." This is the author admitting, in real time, that structure has outrun intention — John has become the protagonist functionally even though the concept was built to resist that. The same day, cf2fc69d drafts the actual Doug Goode confrontation scene (accused of killing "her," of using the tech right now) — meaning as of mid-2023 this material is being written as an *on-page* scene, not held back as prequel-only material. This matters later (see 2026, below).

**Late 2023–early 2024 — quieter, exploratory notes.** 2023-09-17 (Reza and Palm realizing they're on the same side) and 2024-01-14's speculative quantum-entanglement idea ("a character that is just another character but at another point in time, somehow with no knowledge of this") both extend the identity-transfer conceit without hardening it further; neither reappears in any later note.

**Spring–Summer 2024 — external stakes, then the mechanism formalizes.** 2024-05-04 answers "why John specifically" with an institutional, not metaphysical, reason: "Got to be you mate, words from the top" — the Tumult protects him from the Crows because of Harmony politics, a *surface* reason that coexists uneasily with the deeper inheritance story (see Section 3b). Then, across three notes in six days, the thread crystallizes into its clearest pre-2026 form: 2024-09-11 ("Others react to John in a way regardless of what he actually says — subtle but it happens. Not vetko, he sees it and he calls it out") states the core mechanism as prose for the first time; 2024-09-12 ("This story isn't about him / But it will be") turns Vetko's exclusion into an explicit structural promise; and 2024-09-15 recasts Palm — previously "literally an angel" (2023-03-05) — as "the devil in the machine," reducing John himself to "only ever the anonymous messenger between parties." This is a real reversal of Palm's moral valence, not a refinement (see Section 3b). 2024-09-22 then gives Vetko's side of the coin its fullest statement: "his entire personality is not wanting to become irrelevant — dead people don't matter." A week later, 2024-09-29 adds the thread's cleanest foil: Cesira "wants desperately to matter and be the main character... but never actually gets to be central" — a character who wants the property and is refused it, next to John, who has it and doesn't want it.

**Late 2024 — the irrelevance theme turns inward.** Two notes on 2024-11-11 connect the theme to John's psychology rather than just plot mechanics: "It's hard to make friends when you're terrified people won't remember who you are," and the dialogue fragment "They all have a part to play, but you, I don't even know who you are." These are the first notes to make the tenet feel like a wound John carries, not just a fact about the world.

**2025 — a disclaimer, then a real complication to the transfer mechanism.** 2025-02-12's "I most certainly am not the bloke. But I don't think it matters anymore" reads as a late, weary echo of the "no I don't invent it" running gag the audit catalogs — disclaiming the role while conceding the disclaiming is pointless. Then 2025-09-21 substantially reworks how the inheritance actually happens: "Have the main character die for him and he absorbs the main character hood" — the Raven intended to kill Erica outright, as an assassination, specifically *to take her narrative power for the Raven's own use*, but instead she dies alongside John, and it's that accident of proximity — not a clean succession — that hands John the role. This sits oddly against the tenet audit's own framing ("passed from Erica, the Raven / Beck Avon... to John at her death") and against 2023-05-15's simpler mask/gun handoff, since it treats "the Raven" as a distinct actor competing *against* Erica for the property rather than as Erica's own alter ego. No later note reconciles this — flagged in Section 3b.

**Jan 2026 — the synthesis, arriving very late.** 2026-01-18 sets up the final framing: "All want control of narratives" (Vetko, Palm, and IT/Harmony). Then, on a single day, three notes land the thread's mature form. 231ba9b4 is the cleanest thesis statement the whole four-year run produces: "This book is about a world where narrative importance is inherited, and the people who notice that inheritance are the most dangerous ones in the room." 3f5e8ef4 turns that into production rules — "John is the narrative carrier not the emotional centre," "Jones's death is bureaucratic, not heroic," "Palm is a meta-custodian not a saviour" — which quietly resolves the angel/devil whiplash from 2023-03 and 2024-09 by refusing either label. And 9259906e closes a loop that had been open since 2023-07: "John does not change the world. The world keeps changing around him," and — critically — states that John's "real past (Doug Goode, Raven) belongs only in the prequel." That last clause directly reverses 2023-05-15 and 2023-07-11's cf2fc69d, both of which treat the Doug Goode material as on-page scene content for *this* book. As of January 2026, the author's own most recent decision says that material shouldn't be here at all.

## 2. The best decision

**The January 2026 doctrine trio — 231ba9b4, 3f5e8ef4, and 9259906e (all dated 2026-01-24) — is the strongest version of this thread**, and by a wide margin. It's the only point in four years where the author states the rule in one portable sentence ("narrative importance is inherited, and the people who notice that inheritance are the most dangerous ones in the room"), assigns every major character a precise, non-overlapping function (John = carrier not centre; Jones = early emotional centre, bureaucratic death; Palm = meta-custodian not saviour; Harmony = administration not godhood), and — most importantly — resolves the tension the author had been sitting inside since 2023-07-11 ("John is the main character, I guess I wanted that not to be the case though"). The 2026 formulation lets both things be true at once: John is not the *emotional* centre (the author's wish is honored) while still being the *narrative* centre (the structural reality is honored). That's a genuinely better answer than either the 2023 admission or the 2024-09 mechanism note manages alone.

**2023-07-11's admission is the essential precursor, not a rival decision.** It's not marked `isDecision`, and it shouldn't be treated as a destination — it's the problem statement the 2026 doctrine solves.

**One place the later thinking is arguably worse, or at least unreconciled: 2025-09-21's Raven-assassination mechanism.** It's more dramatically loaded than the plain "Erica dies, John inherits" version implied everywhere else, but it recasts "the Raven" as a rival to Erica rather than Erica's own identity, and the January 2026 notes make no reference to it when restating the inheritance rule in its cleanest form. Treat 2025-09-21 as an unresolved variant, not a supersession — pick one before drafting the death scene.

## 3. Against the audits

**(a) Notes that already answer a gap the audit flagged**

- The audit's own summary judgment says the rule is "back-loaded" and never gets a clean early statement. **231ba9b4 (2026-01-24)** — "narrative importance is inherited, and the people who notice that inheritance are the most dangerous ones in the room" — *is* that clean statement, already written, just not yet on the page. It's more precise than any of the three explicit statements the audit locates in the manuscript (pages 525, 614, 618).
- The audit's transfer section asks whether there's "a trace of the mantle passing at her death" and answers itself: **"No. Pure grief-realism; nothing leaves her, nothing lands on John."** The notes show the author already has the mechanism, just never dramatized it: **77ab812b (2023-01-20)**'s shared kill-code implant, **07d685f8 (2023-04-15)**'s "There are no pictures of John Blard. Just one of Erica," and **513170c7 (2023-05-15)**'s "John Blard put down the mask. / And Doug Goode picked up the gun." The gap isn't conceptual — the author solved it in 2023 — it's a dramatization/dosage gap.
- The audit's hardening suggestion #2 wants Palm's recognition (96–97) played as "inspection rather than admiration... a man confirming a hallmark on silver." **1bf41160 (2023-03-05)**'s "Elleray only angel, literally an angel" and the whole 2022-10-27/10-28 cluster of Palm treating meeting John as a foregone verification ("you were done the second you met the fat man") already supply the entity register the audit is asking the prose to earn.
- The audit flags that Vetko's search for the mantle is "present as appetite, never as quest" on the page. The notes already write it as a quest: **3ed01229 (2022-11-28)** — Vetko "searching for John"; **23f1a51b (2024-07-21)** — "I've seen it, I know, I write my own lines"; **5cc13844 (2024-09-22)** — he "disappears to find out how to change... his own death." This is exactly the material needed to convert the rooftop scene from ambush to discovery, per the audit's suggestion #5.
- The audit wants a narrator fingerprint under the early "comic" Palm/John beats so Reza's late thesis (613) doesn't read as retcon. **eb919d4b (2024-09-15)** — "John is only ever the anonymous messenger between parties... palm is the devil in the machine" — gives the structural reason John's charisma is passive rather than earned, which is precisely the connective tissue the audit says is missing between the early farce and the late statement.

**(b) Notes that contradict what the manuscript currently does**

- The audit frames Palm cleanly as "an angel-like entity who knows." The notes don't agree with themselves on this: **1bf41160 (2023-03-05)** calls him "literally an angel," but **eb919d4b (2024-09-15)** calls him "the devil in the machine." The January 2026 doctrine (3f5e8ef4: "Palm is a meta-custodian not a saviour") is a deliberate refusal to pick a side — good for drafting, but it means the audit's flat "angel-like" label is already out of date relative to the author's own latest thinking.
- The **AUTHOR CONSTRAINT** at the top of the audit is explicit: the prequel reveals the Blards' identities, and "this book: hints and weirdness only." **9259906e (2026-01-24)** agrees with that constraint in the most recent note ("his real past (Doug Goode, Raven) belongs only in the prequel") — but this directly contradicts **513170c7 (2023-05-15)** and **cf2fc69d (2023-07-11)**, both of which draft the Doug Goode identity-shift and a full Doug Goode confrontation scene ("I've found a way to get her back," accusations of killing her) as material that happens *in this book*. If either of those 2023 scenes is currently drafted into the manuscript, they conflict with the author's own most recent and most authoritative ruling on where that content belongs.
- **60567ca7 (2023-02-07)** — Palm "forces John into" the Julie plot by lying to the tumtums — sits oddly against the audit's read of Palm as a being who *recognizes* rather than manufactures relevance (audit section 2: "he treats meeting John as verification, not recruitment"). **76ca0ffb (2023-04-03)**'s "Palm starts the codes plot thinking John is his guy when it's actually Lois/Jemima" sharpens this into a real problem: if Palm's targeting began as a mistaken identity, his uncanny "recognition liturgy" (audit item 3, "You were here all along") was originally an error the notes never walk back. Worth a decision before the recognition beats get hardened per the audit's suggestion #2.
- **2025-09-21's** Raven-tries-to-kill-Erica-to-absorb-her-power mechanism treats the Raven as competing *against* Erica for the mantle. The tenet audit's working model — "passed from Erica (the Raven / Beck Avon)... to John" — assumes Raven and Erica are the same figure. These can't both be right as currently worded; pick one before the audit's transfer section gets used to guide revision.

**(c) Ideas that are stale / superseded / safe to bin**

- **7c36844d (2024-01-14)** — the quantum-entanglement "character that is just another character but at another point in time" — is a speculative spark that never recurs. The Doug Goode alter-ego mechanism (2023-05-15) already covers the same territory more concretely and is the one the January 2026 doctrine builds on. Safe to treat as an abandoned branch.
- **76ca0ffb (2023-04-03)**'s Lois/Jemima misdirection for Palm's original target is never mentioned again after this single dense note. Either commit to it as a real complication (see 3b above) or bin it — leaving it unresolved is worse than either choice.
- **e9853da2 (2022-10-27)** — "I need someone to investigate disappearances with" — is tagged to this thread but is really Julie/recruitment texture; it doesn't carry any main-characterness content of its own and can be dropped from this thread's working set.
- **b75c3b6a (2024-05-04)**'s "words from the top" institutional justification for why John is protected is fine as *surface* plot machinery (the Tumult's stated reason) but should not be read as the *real* reason — the January 2026 doctrine's inheritance thesis is the true mechanism. Keep the line as in-world propaganda/cover story, not as competing lore.
