## How to read this audit

Firm-directive notes are your loudest voice; a decision backed only by `musing`/`leaning` notes, or by a single note, is one you *leaned toward*, not one you *settled*. Where the plan says "SETTLED — don't reopen," that's a promise the notes have to keep. Four of these (Baker's death, Julie's discovery, Daz, the Delta-half of Jemima) reopen themselves the moment you check the trail.
